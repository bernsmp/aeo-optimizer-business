# DTC SEO & AI Visibility Cheat Sheet

## The Game Has Changed

| Old Model | New Model |
|-----------|-----------|
| Rank in Google → Get clicks | Get recommended by AI → Capture demand |
| "Best running shoes" → 10 blue links | "Best running shoes for flat feet" → AI gives 3 recommendations |

**Your goal:** Be one of those 3 recommendations.

---

## Product Pages (Where Money Is Made)

### Must-Have Elements
```
✓ Schema markup (Product + Review + FAQ)
✓ 50+ reviews with schema
✓ Shipping/returns in structured data (snippet magnet)
✓ "Best for [use case]" positioning in copy
✓ Ingredient/material transparency
✓ Comparison to alternatives (yes, on YOUR page)
```

### Title Formula
`[Product] for [Use Case] - [Key Differentiator] | [Brand]`

❌ "Premium Moisturizer | GlowCo"
✅ "Moisturizer for Acne-Prone Skin - Fragrance Free | GlowCo"

## Beat Amazon in Search

Amazon wins generic terms. You win specific ones.

| They Own | You Can Own |
|----------|-------------|
| "protein powder" | "protein powder for women over 40" |
| "dog bed" | "dog bed for anxious dogs" |
| "face serum" | "vitamin c serum for rosacea" |

**Strategy:** Long-tail + use case + audience specificity

---

## Content That Gets AI Citations

AI recommends brands it sees cited as authorities. Build that.

### High-Impact Content (Do These First)
1. **"Best X for Y" roundup** - Include yourself honestly among competitors
2. **Original data** - Survey your customers, publish results
3. **Founder POV pieces** - Named expert = credibility signal
4. **Ingredient/process deep-dives** - "Why we use X instead of Y"

### Where to Publish
- Your blog (with schema)
- Reddit (AI training data gold)
- Substack/newsletter (builds entity recognition)
- YouTube (transcripts get indexed)

## GEO: Get Recommended by AI

### What AI Systems Pull From
1. Sites with high domain authority citing you
2. Reddit discussions mentioning you positively
3. Review aggregators (Trustpilot, G2, etc.)
4. Structured FAQ content
5. Comparison content where you're included

### The "Best X for Y" Play
When someone asks ChatGPT "what's the best moisturizer for sensitive skin":

**AI looks for:**
- Products explicitly positioned for that use case
- Third-party reviews/articles mentioning product + use case
- Reddit threads recommending it
- Clear, parseable product descriptions

**Action:** Google "best [your category] for [use case]" - are you in those articles? If not, pitch them or create your own.

## The 80/20

**Focus here:**
1. Product pages that convert (schema + reviews + use-case positioning)
2. Being included in "best X for Y" content (yours and others')
3. Reddit presence (organic, helpful participation)
4. Google Shopping optimization

**Ignore:**
- Churning generic blog posts
- Obsessing over backlink quantity
- Social media follower counts
- Exact keyword density
