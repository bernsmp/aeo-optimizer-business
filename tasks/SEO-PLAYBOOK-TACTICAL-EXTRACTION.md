# SEO Playbook - Tactical Frameworks & Processes
*Extracted from Will Cannon's SEO Playbook (UpLead)*

---

## 1. Site Hierarchy Framework

### Core Principle
Google needs to understand EXACTLY what a business is. Build site hierarchy around ONE clear message repeated everywhere.

**Example:** "UpLead provides real-time verified B2B emails, mobile numbers, and intent data."

### Site Structure
```
Homepage + About Us
├── Product/Service Pages (rank for high-intent searches)
├── Company Pages (pricing, integrations, vs competitors, features)
└── Blog (organized around topic hubs)
```

**Application for clients:** Define their ONE core message and ensure it's consistent across homepage, about, and all product pages.

## 2. Semantic Content Networks (Topic Hubs)

### Structure
```
Pillar Page (Comprehensive guide)
├── Cluster Page 1 (Specific subtopic)
├── Cluster Page 2 (Specific subtopic)
├── Cluster Page 3 (Specific subtopic)
└── Supporting Content (How-tos, tools, comparisons)
```

### Example
```
Lead Generation (Pillar)
├── Lead Generation Tools (Cluster)
├── Lead Generation Strategies (Cluster)
└── How to Start a Lead Generation Business (Cluster)
```

### Linking Rules
- Every cluster page links back to the pillar
- Every pillar links to all clusters
- This creates "topical authority" signals

## 3. Query-Set First Content (Ahrefs Process)

### The Concept
Instead of targeting individual keywords, target entire query SETS. Google shows the same article for multiple related queries.

### Step-by-Step Process
1. **Find best-performing article** for your main keyword in Ahrefs Keywords Explorer
2. **Click on the keyword count** of the top-ranking article
3. **Filter for Position < 15** to see all related queries that article ranks for
4. **Export the list** - these are your query cluster

### Implications
1. Incorporate ALL these keywords in your article
2. Do NOT create separate articles for these keywords (Google expects you to cover them together)

### Example Query Set for "lead generation"
- lead generation process
- lead generation marketing
- lead generation campaigns
- lead generation tips
- lead generation examples

## 4. Content Type Formulas

### A) Info Content (TOFU)
**Purpose:** Target informational keywords, build authority

**Topic Research Process:**
1. Keyword research in Ahrefs for high-volume, low-competition informational queries
2. Use "Clusters by Parent Topic" to group queries for one URL
3. Analyze top 10 results to understand search intent

**Content Strategy:**
- Comprehensive guides (as long as needed to fully answer)
- Cover every subtopic in the query set
- Include case studies and real examples
- Natural product mentions where relevant

---

### B) Listicle Content (MOFU)
**Purpose:** Tool roundups and comparisons, high engagement

**Examples:**
- "25+ Lead Generation Tools"
- "Best 25 Email Finder Tools Compared"

**Why They Work:**
- Easy to scan and compare
- Natural for internal linking
- Perfect for featured snippets
- Easy to update

**The Formula:**
1. Hook with compelling number and clear category
2. Brief intro explaining why these tools matter
3. Each tool: what it does, key features, pricing, pros/cons
4. Include yourself naturally when you fit
5. Clear CTA at the end

---

### C) Vs Content (BOFU) - HIGHEST CONVERTING
**Purpose:** Target direct comparison keywords

**Examples:**
- "UpLead vs. ZoomInfo: Which is Right for Your Business?"
- "Apollo vs ZoomInfo: Comparison Guide"

**Why It Converts:**
- Targets buyers actively evaluating
- Control the narrative around your strengths
- Address objections before they arise

**The Formula:**
1. Neutral introduction acknowledging both solutions
2. Feature-by-feature comparison TABLE
3. Honest assessment of pros/cons for each
4. Specific use cases where each excels
5. Clear recommendation based on different buyer needs

---

### D) Alternative Content (BOFU)
**Purpose:** Capture users looking to switch from competitors

**Examples:**
- "15 Best ZoomInfo Competitors & Alternatives"
- "12 Lusha Alternatives to Consider"

**Why It Works:**
- Captures dissatisfied competitor customers at perfect moment
- Position as obvious choice among alternatives
- Higher commercial intent
- Often easier to rank than head-to-head comparisons

**Content Structure:**
1. Why someone might need an alternative to [Tool X]
2. Key criteria for evaluating alternatives
3. Comprehensive list with your product prominently featured
4. Detailed breakdown of each option's strengths/weaknesses
5. Clear recommendation guiding toward your product

---

### E) Brand/Pricing Content (BOFU)
**Purpose:** Target searches about competitor pricing/features

**Examples:**
- "ZoomInfo Pricing and Plans (2025): Is It Worth It?"
- "Apollo.io Pricing & Plans: Is It The Right Tool?"

**Why It Works:**
- People researching competitor pricing are close to decision
- Position as better value alternative
- Lower competition than direct comparisons
- Builds authority around industry knowledge

## 5. The 3-Dimensional Content Update Framework

### Update Cadence
- **BOFU content** (comparisons, alternatives): Every 3 months
- **MOFU content** (tool lists, how-tos): Every 6 months
- **TOFU content** (educational guides): Every 12 months

### Dimension 1: Missing/Outdated Facts
**What to update:** Statistics, pricing, feature changes, company updates

**ChatGPT Prompt for Research:**
```
"I'm updating an article about [TOPIC] originally published in [DATE]. Please identify what factual information, statistics, or industry developments might be outdated. Focus on:
1. Key statistics that change annually
2. Software features/pricing that frequently update
3. Industry trends that have evolved
4. Regulatory changes or new standards
5. New research or studies published since [DATE]

Provide specific areas to research with suggested authoritative sources."
```

### Dimension 2: Missing Entities & Keywords
**What to update:** New keyword opportunities, related entities, semantic improvements

**Process:**
1. **Ahrefs:** Find top-ranking competitor article → filter for Position 1-15 → see keywords they rank for that you don't
2. **Google Search Console:** Filter Queries for Position 11-50 (keywords you're close to ranking for)
3. **Surfer SEO:** Run article through Content Editor → check Terms section for entities to add

### Dimension 3: Missing/Outdated Topics
**What to update:** New subtopics, emerging trends, additional questions

**ChatGPT Prompt:**
```
"I have an article about [MAIN TOPIC] that covers these sections: [LIST YOUR H2 HEADINGS].

Please analyze what important subtopics I might be missing by:
1. Reviewing what SERP competitors typically cover
2. Identifying related questions users commonly ask
3. Suggesting emerging trends worth adding
4. Recommending case studies or examples to include

For each suggestion, explain why it would strengthen the article.

Article Content:
[ADD YOUR FULL ARTICLE CONTENT]"
```

1. **Homepage** - for overall domain authority
2. **Topic Hub Main Articles** - for topical authority
3. **BOFU Content** - for commercial keyword rankings

### Why This Works
Instead of trying to get links everywhere, concentrate link juice on pages that matter most.

## 7. Link Insertion > Guest Posts

### Why Link Insertions Win
- **Higher response rates** than guest post pitches
- **More cost-effective** than guest posts
- **Better link quality** since content already ranks

### What is a Link Insertion?
Adding your link to an EXISTING relevant article on another site.

### Cold Email Templates

**Template 1: Value-First Approach**
```
Subject: Quick link add to help your [tool/article] get more visibility

Hi [Name],

Writing a comprehensive [tool category] roundup and your [specific tool/article] is a perfect fit for the #[X] spot.

Happy to include it with a solid contextual link: [your article URL]

Think there might be room for a reciprocal mention in your [specific piece of their content]?

Best, [Your name]
```

**Template 2: Guest Post Inclusion**
```
Subject: want to add your link?

[Name],

Just got a guest post approved at [Publication] on [topic] - gets ~[X]k monthly readers.

Perfect spot to mention [their tool/content] with a link. Interested?

If this works well, maybe we can do this regularly - I guest post often.

[Your name]
```

**Template 3: Collaboration Pitch**
```
Subject: ([Your domain] x [Their domain])

Hi [Name],

Run [YourSite.com] ([brief description]) - noticed we cover similar ground as [TheirSite.com].

Think our audiences would benefit from cross-mentions. You game for some mutual linking/collaboration?

Worth a quick chat?

[Your name]
```

### Collaboration Offer (What to give back)
1. Link back from your relevant content
2. Share their article in your newsletter
3. Amplify on social channels
4. Feature them as expert in future content

## 8. Technical SEO That Matters

### URL Structure
- **Bad:** uplead.com/post/12345/how-to-generate-leads
- **Good:** uplead.com/lead-generation-strategies

Keep URLs short, descriptive, keyword-focused.

### Meta Title Formula
Descriptive, clear titles first. CTAs belong in descriptions, not titles.

**Examples:**
- "23 Proven Lead Generation Strategies to Grow Quickly"
- "UpLead vs. ZoomInfo: Which is Right for Your Business?"

### Meta Descriptions
Sales copy that matches query intent:
```
"Looking for lead generation strategies? Here are 23 proven methods and techniques you haven't heard of that you can implement today. Use them and generate more prospects"
```

### Alt Text
- **Bad:** alt="screenshot-1.png"
- **Good:** alt="UpLead contact search filters showing job title and location options"

### Image Optimization
- Compress every image to under 100KB (TinyPNG)
- Descriptive alt text with target keyword when relevant

### Core Web Vitals
- Target LCP ≤ 2.5 seconds
- Don't lazy-load the LCP image - deliver it early (fetchpriority=high, preload)
- Defer non-critical JavaScript
- Prefer server-rendered HTML for critical copy

## 13. HARO / Help a B2B Writer Mastery

### The 3-Sentence Rule
1. Direct answer to their question with specific stat/example
2. Your unique angle or contrarian insight
3. Your credentials (brief)

### Example Response
```
Hi [Name],

Email verification accuracy drops 15-20% every 6 months due to job changes and email updates, based on our analysis of 160M contacts. The biggest mistake companies make is verifying emails once during import instead of real-time verification at point of use.

I'm Will Cannon, CEO of UpLead, where we process 2M+ email verifications monthly.

About me:
CEO & Founder, UpLead
LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/will-cannon/
Website: uplead.com
High-res headshot: [URL]
Email: will@uplead.com
```

### Pro Tips
- **Respond within 2 hours** (most journalists pick from first 10 responses)
- Include 1 relevant stat they can cite
- Never pitch product directly in HARO responses
- Follow up with additional data if they use your quote

## 14. Step-by-Step Playbooks

### How to Build a Topic Hub (Query-Set First)
1. From seed topic, pull comprehensive keyword list in Ahrefs
2. Use Clusters by Parent Topic to group terms for one URL
3. Map clusters to content types (Pillar, Cluster, Support)
4. Draft canonical H1 for pillar; outline H2s/H3s to cover full query set
5. Add direct answer box + evidence block to each page
6. Publish pillar + 2-4 clusters together; interlink across the hub
7. Re-crawl & measure monthly

### Internal Linking Sprint (1-2 Weeks)
1. Export all URLs + target topics
2. For each hub, ensure pillar ↔ cluster links exist (2-4 contextual links per page)
3. Fix anchor text to be descriptive (avoid "click here")
4. Add "Related reading" boxes to top 30 URLs
5. Re-crawl site; check with GSC

### LCP Improvement Sprint (1 Week)
1. Identify LCP element on top 20 pages (usually hero image/heading)
2. Optimize LCP image: fetchpriority=high, correct dimensions, compress, next-gen formats
3. Don't lazy-load the LCP image
4. Inline critical CSS; defer non-critical JS; lazy-load below-fold images
5. Measure in PageSpeed Insights; target ≤2.5s

### AIO-Ready Content Module (Add to Every Post)
1. Direct Answer section (2-3 sentences)
2. Evidence block with 1-3 third-party citations
3. Mini-FAQ section (3 Q&As) with FAQ schema
4. "Last updated [Month Year]" timestamp
5. Sources footer with named sources

## 15. Key Metrics Framework

### Vanity Metrics (Ignore)
- Total traffic
- Keyword rankings
- Domain authority
- Blog posts published

### Metrics That Matter
- Organic conversion rate
- Revenue from non-branded organic
- Brand mentions in AI responses
- Products appearing in "best X" results

---

## 16. The 80/20 Summary

### Focus Here
1. Product pages that convert (schema + reviews + use-case positioning)
2. Being included in "best X for Y" content (yours and others')
3. Reddit presence (organic, helpful participation)
4. Google Shopping optimization (for e-commerce)

### Ignore
- Churning generic blog posts
- Obsessing over backlink quantity
- Social media follower counts
- Exact keyword density
