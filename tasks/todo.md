## Strategic Framework: Partner vs Provider

### The Problem with "Service Provider" Positioning
- Transaction-based: deliver → invoice → goodbye
- Commoditized: competing on price
- No recurring value: one-time fix

### Strategic Partner Positioning
- Relationship-based: diagnose → roadmap → measure → grow
- Value-driven: competing on outcomes
- Ongoing partnership: continuous improvement

## The MAIX AEO Methodology

### Core Insight: SEO + AEO = Compound Visibility

```
Traditional SEO (Foundation)
        ↓
    Powers
        ↓
AEO (Amplifier) → AI Citations → Brand Authority
        ↓
    Reinforces
        ↓
Traditional SEO (Higher rankings)
```

### Why This Matters
- SEO gets you ranked
- AEO gets you CITED
- Being cited builds authority
- Authority improves rankings
- **Flywheel effect**
