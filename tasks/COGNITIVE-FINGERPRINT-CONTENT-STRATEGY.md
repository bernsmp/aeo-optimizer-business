## Step 1: Define the ONE Core Message

**Current positioning from site:**
> "Making invisible expertise visible" - extracting the "invisible 90% of what makes you valuable"

**Refined core message for SEO:**
> "Cognitive Fingerprint extracts tacit expertise from consultants, coaches, and experts - turning unconscious competence into named, monetizable frameworks without forcing you to invent from scratch."

**Key differentiator:**
Unlike "create your signature framework" courses that ask you to BUILD from nothing, CF EXTRACTS from your existing work (transcripts, decisions, client interactions).

## Key Strategic Insight

**The "Create vs Extract" Positioning:**

Most content in this space tells experts to CREATE their framework from scratch. This is hard and leads to generic frameworks.

CF's angle is EXTRACTION - analyzing what you already do to find the patterns.

Every piece of content should reinforce this distinction:
- "You don't need to invent a framework. You already have one."
- "The framework exists in your work. We just make it visible."
- "Creation is hard. Extraction is discovery."

This creates a unique position in a crowded "signature framework" space.
