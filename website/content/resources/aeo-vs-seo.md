# AEO vs SEO: What's the Difference?

## The Key Differences

While SEO and AEO both aim to increase your visibility, they target different search experiences:

### Traditional SEO
- Focuses on ranking in search results
- Optimizes for Google's algorithm
- Takes weeks/months to see results
- Requires backlinks and domain authority

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
- Focuses on being cited by AI assistants
- Optimizes for AI understanding
- Can see results in 2-7 days
- Requires structured data and clear answers

## Why You Need Both

The best strategy combines both approaches:

1. **SEO** gets you ranked in search results
2. **AEO** gets you cited by AI assistants
3. **Together** they maximize your visibility across all search experiences

## Getting Started

Start with the technical foundation that helps both:
- Schema markup (helps SEO + AEO)
- FAQ pages (helps SEO + AEO)
- Clear, answer-focused content (helps SEO + AEO)

Then optimize specifically for each:
- SEO: Backlinks, domain authority, keyword optimization
- AEO: AI crawler files, citation optimization, answer formatting
