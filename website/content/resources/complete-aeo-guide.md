# Complete AEO Guide

## Introduction

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of optimizing your website so that AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI can find, understand, and cite your content when answering user queries.

## What is AEO?

AEO differs from traditional SEO in several key ways:

1. **AI-first indexing**: AI search engines can index content much faster (often within 2-7 days)
2. **Citation-focused**: The goal is to be cited, not just ranked
3. **Answer-oriented**: Content must provide clear, direct answers to questions
4. **Structured data**: Schema markup and structured content are essential

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Technical Foundation

Start by adding structured data to your website:

- Organization schema
- FAQ schema
- Service/Product schema
- Article schema

### Step 2: Content Optimization

Optimize your content for AI understanding:

- Write clear, direct answers
- Use FAQ format for common questions
- Structure content with headings
- Include relevant keywords naturally

### Step 3: AI Crawler Files

Add an `llms.txt` file to help AI crawlers understand your site structure and key pages.
