# AEO Term Decision: Based on SEMDash Research

## Research Finding

**"AEO" by itself barely comes up in SEMDash**

This confirms:
- ❌ Very low search volume (<100/month likely)
- ❌ People don't search for "AEO"
- ❌ Not a viable primary term for SEO/content

---

## What This Means

### Don't Use "AEO" as Primary Term

**Why:**
- Nobody searches for it
- Ranking #1 won't drive traffic
- Wasted SEO effort
- No proven demand

### But You Can Still Use "AEO" Strategically

**As:**
- ✅ Brand name / method name
- ✅ Secondary term in content
- ✅ Category you're building
- ✅ Differentiator ("We call it AEO")

---

## Revised Strategy

### Primary Terms (Use These Instead)

**Based on SEMDash research, use:**

1. **"AI SEO"** (if it has volume)
   - Primary term for content
   - Primary term for SEO
   - What people actually search

2. **"ChatGPT SEO"** (if growing)
   - Secondary term
   - Less competitive
   - More specific

3. **"Answer Engine Optimization"** (full phrase)
   - Use in content naturally
   - Explains what "AEO" means
   - Long-tail keyword

### How to Use "AEO"

**In content:**
- Title: "AI SEO: The Complete Guide to Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)"
- Content: "AI SEO (we call it AEO) is..."
- You get traffic from "AI SEO" searches
- You build "AEO" brand over time

**In service:**
- Service name: "AEO Services" (brand)
- But optimize for: "AI SEO services" (traffic)
- Best of both worlds
