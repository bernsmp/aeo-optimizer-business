# AEO Insights from Nick Lafferty (Profound) - Hypergrowth Leadership Podcast

## 🎯 Key Insights

### 1. Terminology: "Agent Experience" (AX)

**Nick's Take:**
- Profound prefers "Agent Experience" (AX) over AEO/GEO
- "Who is everyone's new customer? It's AI. It's an AI bot visiting your website."
- Designing for bots vs humans = fundamentally different
- Bots don't care about design - design can get in the way

**Your Framework Implication:**
- ✅ Your Layer 1 (Technical Foundation) aligns with this
- ✅ Technical optimization matters more than design for AI
- ✅ "Agent Experience" could be alternative positioning

---

### 2. Prompts vs Keywords (Critical Difference)

**Nick's Key Insight:**
- **Average prompt:** 20+ words
- **Average Google search:** 3-5 words
- **Step Zero:** Figure out which prompts to track (not keywords)

**How to Find Prompts:**
1. **Lead form question:** "How did you hear about us?" → Add "ChatGPT/LLM" option
2. **Ask customers:** "What prompt did you use? Copy/paste it"
3. **Internal exercise:** Have team pretend to be customers, ask ChatGPT questions
4. **Common themes:** Build prompts from patterns

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Layer 1: Long-tail question content (25-word queries) = Validated
- ✅ Need to emphasize: Track prompts, not keywords

---

### 3. Visibility Tracking (Step One)

**Nick's Approach:**
- Track visibility manually or with tools (Profound automates)
- Calculate visibility score: "Visible in 2 out of 10 prompts" vs "9 out of 10"
- **Offense strategy:** 20% visibility → Create new content, optimize existing
- **Defense strategy:** 90% visibility → Protect cited pages, don't screw up

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Layer 2: Visibility Strategy = Validated
- ✅ Add: Visibility tracking as first step
- ✅ Add: Offense vs defense strategy

---

### 4. Traffic Doesn't Matter As Much

**Nick's Key Quote:**
*"You don't need a ton of existing traffic to a page or website or even a domain for it to be cited very often in AI search."*

**Examples:**
- Reddit thread from 2 years ago can be cited most
- Subpages with Q&A format rank higher
- Comparison pages rank higher
- Even if they're not highest traffic sites

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Early stage advantage = Validated
- ✅ Long-tail content = Validated
- ✅ Help center optimization = Validated

---

### 5. Sites That Index Well (Updated List)

**Nick's Data (from 30-50M prompts):**
1. **Wikipedia** - 45%+ of ChatGPT answers
2. **Reddit** - Very high citation rate
3. **Forbes** - Q&A format, not behind paywall
4. **G2, TechRadar** - Comparison sites
5. **Affiliate sites** - Wirecutter, etc. (industry dependent)
6. **Product sites** - With open crawling

**Industry Differences:**
- **B2B:** NerdWallet, TechRadar, G2
- **B2C:** Affiliate sites (Wirecutter for mattresses)
- **Fintech/B2B SaaS:** Leading adoption

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Layer 2: Wikipedia mentions (hard to get)
- ✅ Layer 2: Reddit = Validated (priority)
- ✅ Layer 2: Affiliates = Validated (Dotdash/Meredith, Forbes)
- ✅ Add: G2, TechRadar for B2B

---

### 6. Content Velocity (HUGE Insight)

**Nick's Key Quote:**
*"Velocity is probably the biggest thing to anchor on. ChatGPT can pick up new content within 2 days."*

**Comparison:**
- **Google:** Weeks/months to index, crack top 20
- **ChatGPT:** 2 days to pick up new content
- **Why:** LLMs do web search, not just training data

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Add: Velocity advantage
- ✅ Early stage can win quickly = Validated
- ✅ Content can work in 2 days vs weeks/months

---

### 7. Comparison Pages Are Essential

**Nick's Key Quote:**
*"Comparison pages are by far the best place to start. If you don't have comparative content, ChatGPT will either make it up or pull from your competitor pages."*

**CEO Pushback:**
- CEOs don't want to name competitors on their site
- **Reality:** Customers already know competitors (G2, etc.)
- **Benefit:** You control the narrative

**Postmark Example:**
- Best form of comparative content Nick has seen
- Honest, truthful, accurate alternatives page

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Layer 1: Comparison content = Add
- ✅ Layer 2: Off-property comparisons = Add

---

### 8. Off-Property Content Is More Authoritative

**Nick's Strategy:**
- Off-property content is more authoritative
- More scalable: Network of 5-10 partner sites
- Profound partners with brands/agencies
- Pay for content, freelance writer, editorial approval
- "Takes time but absolutely worth doing"

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Layer 2: Off-property content = Add
- ✅ Partner network strategy = Add

### 9. Traffic & Conversion Rates

**Nick's Data:**
- **Profound:** 15% of leads from ChatGPT
- **Vercel:** 10% of signups from ChatGPT
- **Most common journey:** ChatGPT → Google brand search → Direct
- **Siege Media study:** Being visible in AI results increases branded search 10-15%
- **Profound research (2,000 people):** 80% of shoppers using answer engines rely on it for half their decision-making
- **Conversion rates:** Higher (people more educated)

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Higher conversion rates = Validated (Ethan also said 6x)
- ✅ Branded search increase = Add to value prop
