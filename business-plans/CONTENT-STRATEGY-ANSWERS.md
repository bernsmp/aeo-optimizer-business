## 📝 Content Velocity vs Depth

**Answer: Mix of both, with velocity as proof point**

### Strategy: **70% Velocity / 30% Depth**

**High-Volume Content (70%):**
- Demonstrate content velocity strategy you're recommending
- Show that you practice what you preach
- Create comparison pages quickly (essential content type)
- Long-tail question content (25-word queries)
- Q&A format content
- Help center optimization examples
- **Goal:** Prove the velocity advantage works

**Deep, Researched Pieces (30%):**
- Technical deep-dives (schema implementation, AI crawlers)
- Framework explanations (why Technical → Visibility → Brand)
- Industry research synthesis
- Case studies (when you have them)
- **Goal:** Establish technical expertise and thought leadership

**Content Mix Example:**
- **Week 1:** 5 quick comparison pages (velocity)
- **Week 2:** 1 deep technical guide (depth)
- **Week 3:** 10 Q&A format pages (velocity)
- **Week 4:** 1 framework explanation piece (depth)

**Why This Ratio:**
- Velocity proves your strategy works
- Depth establishes credibility
- Early stage companies need to see fast results
- Technical depth differentiates from "hack" sellers
