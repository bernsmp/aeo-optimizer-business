# Deep Research Prompt for Gemini: AEO Knowledge Base

## 🎯 Primary Research Prompt

Copy and paste this into Gemini for comprehensive AEO research:

---

```
I'm building a knowledge base for an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) service business. I need comprehensive research across multiple dimensions. Please conduct deep research and provide detailed, actionable insights.

## Research Objectives

### 1. Industry Landscape & Terminology
- What are the different terms used? (AEO, GEO, Agent Experience/AX, etc.)
- Who coined these terms and when?
- What are the semantic differences between terms?
- Which term is gaining the most traction in 2024-2025?
- What are industry leaders saying about terminology?
- Are there any trademark or branding considerations?

### 2. Market Size & Growth
- What is the current market size for AEO/GEO services?
- What is the projected growth rate?
- How many businesses are actively optimizing for AI search?
- What percentage of businesses are aware of AEO?
- What are the adoption rates by industry?
- What are the key growth drivers?

### 3. Competitor Analysis
- Who are the major players in AEO/GEO services?
- What are their positioning strategies?
- What are their pricing models?
- What are their key differentiators?
- What are their weaknesses or gaps?
- Which companies have shut down or pivoted? Why?
- What are the most successful AEO case studies publicly available?

### 4. Technical Implementation
- What are the core technical requirements for AEO?
- What schema markup types are most effective?
- How do robots.txt and llms.txt work for AI crawlers?
- What are the best practices for content structure?
- What technical SEO elements translate to AEO?
- What are common technical mistakes?
- What tools exist for AEO technical audits?

### 5. Content Strategy
- What types of content perform best in AI search?
- What is the optimal content format (Q&A, comparisons, lists, etc.)?
- How long should content be?
- What is the role of comparison pages?
- How important is off-property content?
- What are the best practices for long-tail question content?
- How do help centers factor into AEO?

### 6. Citation Sources & Visibility
- Which websites are most cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini?
- What is the citation distribution by industry (B2B vs B2C)?
- How important is Wikipedia for AEO?
- What is the role of Reddit in AI citations?
- Which affiliate sites are most influential?
- How do YouTube/Vimeo videos factor in?
- What is the impact of G2, TechRadar, Forbes, etc.?
- What are the citation patterns by query type?

### 7. Prompt Research & Tracking
- How do prompts differ from keywords?
- What is the average prompt length?
- How do you identify which prompts to track?
- What tools exist for prompt tracking?
- How do you measure prompt volume?
- What are the best practices for prompt research?
- How do you track visibility across different LLMs?

### 8. Conversion & Attribution
- What are typical conversion rates from AI search traffic?
- How do conversion rates compare to Google search?
- What are the attribution challenges?
- How do you measure AI search impact?
- What is the typical customer journey from AI search?
- How does AI visibility impact branded search?

### 9. Industry-Specific Insights
- How does AEO differ for B2B SaaS vs B2C e-commerce?
- What are the best practices for fintech companies?
- How do different industries approach AEO?
- What are industry-specific citation sources?
- What are industry-specific content strategies?

### 10. Tools & Resources
- What tools exist for AEO optimization?
- What are the pricing models for AEO tools?
- What are the limitations of current tools?
- What are the best free resources?
- What communities or forums discuss AEO?
- What are the best blogs, podcasts, newsletters?

### 11. Case Studies & Results
- What are publicly available AEO case studies?
- What results have companies achieved?
- How quickly do results appear?
- What are the typical timelines?
- What are the most impressive success stories?
- What are common failure patterns?

### 12. Trends & Future Predictions
- What are the emerging trends in AEO?
- How is the landscape evolving?
- What are predictions for 2025-2026?
- How will AI search platforms evolve?
- What new opportunities are emerging?
- What threats or challenges are on the horizon?

### 13. Pricing & Business Models
- What are typical pricing models for AEO services?
- What is the price range for different service levels?
- What are the pricing models for AEO tools/SaaS?
- What is the market rate for AEO consultants?
- What are the typical engagement structures?

### 14. Target Customer Profiles
- Who is actively seeking AEO services?
- What are their pain points?
- What are their objections?
- What is their decision-making process?
- What are their budget ranges?
- What are their success criteria?

### 15. Messaging & Positioning
- What messaging resonates with potential customers?
- What are common misconceptions about AEO?
- What objections do people have?
- What are the best value propositions?
- How do successful companies position AEO services?
- What are the most effective sales arguments?

## Research Format

For each section, please provide:
1. **Key Findings** - Main insights
2. **Data Points** - Specific numbers, statistics, percentages
3. **Examples** - Real-world examples, case studies, quotes
4. **Sources** - Where the information comes from
5. **Actionable Insights** - How to apply this knowledge
6. **Gaps** - What information is missing or unclear

## Depth Requirements

- Go beyond surface-level information
- Include recent data (2024-2025)
- Cite specific sources when possible
- Include conflicting viewpoints
- Note areas where information is limited
- Provide context and nuance

## Output Format

Please organize the research in a structured format that can be easily referenced and built upon. Include:
- Executive summary
- Detailed findings by section
- Key quotes and data points
- Source citations
- Actionable recommendations
- Questions for further research

Begin with the most critical sections first (Industry Landscape, Competitor Analysis, Technical Implementation) and work through systematically.
```
