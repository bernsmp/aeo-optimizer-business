# DELIVERABLE 2: "FAILED HACKS" DATABASE

## Evidence-Based Failed Tactics with Sources

### Category A: Manipulation Tactics (Actively Harmful)

#### 1. **Keyword Stuffing**
- **What it is**: Repeating target keywords excessively throughout content
- **Why it failed**: AI models identify unnatural keyword density as manipulation
- **Evidence**: **-10% visibility decrease** across all content categories
- **Study**: Princeton University GEO research (arXiv:2311.09735)
- **When**: Never worked for GEO; actively counterproductive
- **What works instead**: Natural keyword use + citations, statistics, expert quotes (30-40% boost)
- **Source**: https://www.maximuslabs.ai/generative-engine-optimization/geo-experimental-techniques

#### 2. **Mass AI-Generated Content Without Human Curation**
- **What it is**: Auto-generating thousands of low-quality articles using LLMs
- **Why it failed**: Creates "model collapse"; lacks authentic E-E-A-T signals
- **Evidence**: **40% worse performance** than human-written alternatives; Sports Illustrated caught with fake AI profiles
- **Penalties**: De-indexing, manual penalties, algorithmic downgrading
- **When**: Google March 2024 update specifically targeted this
- **What works instead**: AI as starting point + human expertise, original research, personal experience
- **Source**: https://searchengineland.com/black-hat-geo-pay-attention-463684

#### 3. **Fake E-E-A-T Signals**
- **What it is**: Synthetic author personas with AI-generated photos/fake credentials; mass-produced fake reviews
- **Why it failed**: AI platforms actively detect fabricated authority signals
- **Evidence**: "Black hat GEO now fabricates these signals using AI to create synthetic author personas"
- **Penalties**: Permanent trust erosion, potential de-indexing, loss of credibility
- **When**: Being actively detected 2024-2025
- **What works instead**: Real authors with verifiable credentials, genuine expertise
- **Source**: https://searchengineland.com/black-hat-geo-pay-attention-463684

#### 4. **The Lorelight Revelation: "There's No Such Thing as GEO Strategy"**
- **What it is**: Building standalone GEO tracking/monitoring tools without strategic implementation
- **Why it failed**: "Insights had zero impact on behavior"; customers churned because tools showed problem but didn't solve it
- **Evidence**: Founder shut down platform realizing "there's no such thing as 'GEO strategy' separate from brand building"
- **Critical insight**: "There was no secret formula. No hidden hack. No special optimization technique"
- **What actually works**: Quality content, authoritative publication mentions, strong reputation, genuine expertise
- **Source**: https://growwithless.com/shutting-down-lorelight/

### Critical Statistics on Failure Rates
- **95%** of GEO initiatives produce zero measurable results
- **-10%** visibility decrease from keyword stuffing (Princeton)
- **-40%** performance penalty for purely AI-generated content
- **0-2%** improvement from authoritative tone alone without evidence
- **Only 30%** of enterprises believe GEO outcomes are "truly measurable"
