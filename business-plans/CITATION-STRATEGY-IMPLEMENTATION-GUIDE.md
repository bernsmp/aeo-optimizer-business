# Citation Strategy Implementation Guide
## How to Execute Layer 2: Visibility Strategy

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for implementing citation strategy based on industry research from Ethan Smith (Graphite) and Nick Lafferty (Profound).

---

1. **YouTube/Vimeo Videos** (PRIORITY - especially B2B)
2. **Reddit Authentic Engagement** (PRIORITY)
3. **Affiliate Partnerships** (Dotdash/Meredith, Forbes, TechRadar)
4. **PR & Media Mentions** (Industry publications)
5. **Your Own Site** (Traditional SEO content)

## 1. YouTube/Vimeo Video Strategy

### Why It Works
- **Ethan Smith:** "Easy, no community moderation"
- **Ethan Smith:** "Especially effective for B2B - less competition"
- **Nick Lafferty:** Videos are heavily cited by AI, especially for specific B2B topics
- **Early stage advantage:** Can create videos quickly, no domain authority needed

### Step-by-Step Implementation

#### Step 1: Identify Target Prompts/Questions
**Goal:** Find the 25-word prompts that lead to citations you want

**How to do it:**
1. **Ask customers:** "What prompt did you use in ChatGPT? Copy/paste it"
   - Add to lead form: "How did you hear about us?" → Add "ChatGPT/LLM" option
   - In sales calls: "What question did you ask ChatGPT?"
   - In support: "What were you trying to find?"

2. **Internal exercise:** Have your team pretend to be customers
   - Each person asks ChatGPT 5 questions a customer would ask
   - Copy/paste the exact prompts
   - Look for patterns

3. **Competitor analysis:** 
   - Search ChatGPT/Perplexity for prompts about your industry
   - See which videos are cited
   - Note the prompt patterns

4. **Common themes:** Build prompts from patterns
   - "How does [your product] compare to [competitor]?"
   - "What are the best [use cases] for [your product]?"
   - "How do I [specific task] with [your product]?"

**Output:** List of 20-30 target prompts (25-word questions)

---

#### Step 2: Create Video Content Strategy
**Goal:** Create videos that answer target prompts

**Video Types (Prioritize):**
1. **Comparison videos** (highest priority)
   - "X vs Y: Which is Better for [Use Case]?"
   - "We Compared [Product] vs [Competitor] - Here's What We Found"
   - Example: "AEO vs SEO: What Actually Works in 2024"

2. **How-to videos** (specific use cases)
   - "How to [Specific Task] with [Your Product]"
   - "Complete Guide to [Feature] for [Industry]"
   - Example: "How to Set Up AEO Schema Markup in 10 Minutes"

3. **Feature/Integration deep-dives
   - "Does [Product] Integrate with [Tool]?"
   - "How [Feature] Works: Complete Walkthrough"
   - Example: "How AEO Visibility Tracking Works (Offense vs Defense)"

4. **Long-tail question videos** (25-word queries)
   - Answer specific, detailed questions
   - Cover follow-up questions in same video
   - Example: "What's the Difference Between AEO and GEO, and Which Should You Focus On?"

**Video Format:**
- **Length:** 5-15 minutes (sweet spot for AI citations)
- **Style:** Screen recording + talking head (for B2B)
- **Structure:**
  - Hook (answer the question in first 30 seconds)
  - Main content (detailed answer)
  - Follow-up questions (address related questions)
  - CTA (link to your site)

**Production Quality:**
- **Good enough:** Clear audio, decent lighting, screen recording
- **Don't overthink:** AI doesn't care about production value
- **Focus on:** Answering the question clearly and completely

---

#### Step 3: Optimize Videos for AI Discovery

**Title Optimization:**
- Include the exact prompt/question
- Example: "How to Optimize Your Website for ChatGPT and Perplexity: Complete AEO Guide"
- Use natural language (25-word queries)

**Description Optimization:**
- Answer the question in first 200 words
- Include all follow-up questions
- Use natural language, not keyword stuffing
- Include timestamps for different questions

**Tags:**
- Use natural language tags
- Include variations of the prompt
- Tag related questions

**Transcript:**
- Upload full transcript (YouTube auto-generates, but upload yours)
- Include all questions answered
- Natural language, not keyword-stuffed

---

#### Step 4: Distribution Strategy

**Publishing Schedule:**
- **Frequency:** 1-2 videos per week (velocity advantage)
- **Timing:** Publish when you create them (AI indexes in 2 days)
- **Consistency:** More important than perfection

**Cross-Promotion:**
- Share on LinkedIn (especially for B2B)
- Include in email newsletter
- Link from relevant blog posts
- Share in relevant communities (authentically)

**Measurement:**
- Track which videos get cited in ChatGPT/Perplexity
- Ask customers: "Which video did you see?"
- Monitor prompt patterns that lead to citations

---

### YouTube Strategy Checklist

- [ ] Identified 20-30 target prompts/questions
- [ ] Created video content calendar (comparison, how-to, deep-dives)
- [ ] Set up YouTube channel (or optimized existing)
- [ ] Created first comparison video
- [ ] Created first how-to video
- [ ] Optimized titles/descriptions for natural language queries
- [ ] Uploaded transcripts
- [ ] Set publishing schedule (1-2 videos/week)
- [ ] Set up tracking (which prompts lead to citations)

## 2. Reddit Authentic Engagement Strategy

### Why It Works
- **Ethan Smith:** "HUGE opportunity, but spam doesn't work"
- **Nick Lafferty:** Reddit is one of the most cited sources in LLMs
- **Key insight:** Even 5 authentic comments can work
- **Warning:** Spam gets banned - community manages this well

### Step-by-Step Implementation

#### Step 1: Find Citation Threads
**Goal:** Identify Reddit threads that are part of citations you want to show up in

**How to do it:**
1. **Search ChatGPT/Perplexity** for prompts about your industry/product
   - Copy the exact prompt
   - See which Reddit threads are cited
   - Note the subreddits and thread topics

2. **Search Reddit directly** for relevant discussions
   - Use Reddit search: `site:reddit.com [your topic]`
   - Find threads asking questions you can answer
   - Look for threads with engagement (comments, upvotes)

3. **Monitor relevant subreddits:**
   - Join subreddits where your customers hang out
   - Set up alerts for keywords/questions
   - Example: r/SaaS, r/entrepreneur, r/marketing (for B2B)

4. **Track competitor mentions:**
   - Search for competitor names + "vs" or "alternative"
   - Find threads comparing products
   - These are citation opportunities

**Output:** List of 10-20 target threads/subreddits

---

#### Step 2: Authentic Engagement Strategy
**Goal:** Provide value while being transparent about who you are

**The Formula (From Ethan Smith):**
1. **Say who you are:** "I'm [Name], I work at [Company]"
2. **Say where you work:** "We build [Product]"
3. **Give useful information:** Answer the question with real value
4. **Be transparent:** Don't hide your affiliation
5. **Don't spam:** Quality over quantity

**Example Good Comment:**
```
"I'm Max, I work at AEO Optimizer. We help companies get visible in ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Here's what we've found works:

1. Technical foundation first (schema markup, AI crawlers)
2. YouTube videos are heavily cited (especially for B2B)
3. Reddit threads like this one get cited often

The key is being mentioned across multiple sources. One thing that doesn't work: spam. AI detects it.

Happy to answer questions if helpful."
```

**Example Bad Comment (Spam):**
```
"Check out our AEO service! We can help you rank in ChatGPT. Visit our website now!"
```
*(This gets banned)*

---

#### Step 3: Engagement Tactics

**When to Engage:**
- Thread is asking a question you can answer
- Thread is comparing products (including yours)
- Thread is discussing problems your product solves
- Thread has engagement (not dead threads)

**What to Provide:**
- **Real answers:** Don't just promote, actually help
- **Specific examples:** Use your experience/case studies
- **Honest comparisons:** If competitor is better for X, say so
- **Follow-up:** Answer questions in replies

**Frequency:**
- **Ethan Smith:** "Even 5 comments can work"
- **Quality over quantity:** Better to have 5 great comments than 50 spammy ones
- **Consistency:** Engage regularly, not all at once

**Reddit Best Practices:**
- **Read the rules:** Each subreddit has rules - follow them
- **Be helpful first:** Answer questions before promoting
- **Engage in discussion:** Don't just drop links
- **Build karma:** Participate in subreddit community
- **Don't delete:** If comment gets downvoted, learn and improve

---

#### Step 4: Tracking & Measurement

**What to Track:**
- Which threads you've engaged in
- Which threads get cited in ChatGPT/Perplexity
- Which prompts lead to Reddit citations
- Your comment performance (upvotes, replies)

**Tools:**
- **Reddit:** Save threads you've engaged in
- **ChatGPT/Perplexity:** Test prompts to see if your threads are cited
- **Spreadsheet:** Track threads, dates, prompts, citations

**Measurement:**
- **Goal:** Get your Reddit comments cited in AI answers
- **Success metric:** "X% of target prompts cite Reddit threads where we engaged"
- **Timeline:** Results can happen quickly (2 days for new content)

---

### Reddit Strategy Checklist

- [ ] Identified 10-20 target threads/subreddits
- [ ] Set up Reddit account (or optimized existing)
- [ ] Read subreddit rules for target communities
- [ ] Created authentic engagement template
- [ ] Engaged in first 5 threads (quality comments)
- [ ] Set up tracking spreadsheet
- [ ] Tested prompts to see if threads are cited
- [ ] Established engagement schedule (regular, not spammy)

## 3. Affiliate Partnerships Strategy

### Why It Works
- **Ethan Smith:** Dotdash/Meredith (Good Housekeeping, AllRecipes, Investopedia), Forbes, TechRadar
- **Nick Lafferty:** G2, TechRadar for B2B; Wirecutter for B2C
- **Industry differences:** B2B vs B2C have different affiliate sites
- **Reality:** May require budget for paid mentions

### Step-by-Step Implementation

#### Step 1: Identify Target Affiliate Sites

**B2B SaaS Affiliate Sites:**
- **G2** - Software reviews and comparisons
- **TechRadar** - Tech product reviews
- **Forbes** - Business/tech coverage (Q&A format, not behind paywall)
- **NerdWallet** - Fintech/B2B SaaS
- **Capterra** - Software directory
- **GetApp** - Software reviews

**B2C Affiliate Sites:**
- **Wirecutter** (NY Times) - Product reviews
- **Dotdash/Meredith** - Good Housekeeping, AllRecipes, Investopedia
- **CNET** - Tech product reviews
- **Consumer Reports** - Product comparisons

**Your Industry:** Identify which affiliate sites matter for your space

---

#### Step 2: Partnership Approaches

**Option A: Paid Mentions (If Budget Allows)**
- **What it is:** Pay for editorial mentions/reviews
- **How it works:** Contact affiliate site, propose paid content
- **Cost:** Varies by site (can be $500-$5,000+)
- **Best for:** Established companies with budget

**Option B: Free/Organic Mentions**
- **What it is:** Get mentioned through PR, reviews, comparisons
- **How it works:** 
  - Submit product for review
  - Respond to journalist queries (Help a Reporter Out - HARO)
  - Provide expert quotes for articles
  - Build relationships with writers
- **Cost:** Time, not money
- **Best for:** Early-stage companies

**Option C: Comparison Pages**
- **What it is:** Create comparison content on affiliate sites
- **How it works:**
  - Some sites allow user-generated comparisons
  - G2 allows detailed comparisons
  - Provide accurate, honest comparisons
- **Cost:** Time to create content
- **Best for:** All companies

---

#### Step 3: Implementation Steps

**For Paid Mentions:**
1. **Research:** Find contact info for affiliate sites
2. **Pitch:** Create pitch deck with product info, value prop
3. **Negotiate:** Discuss pricing, editorial control, placement
4. **Deliver:** Provide content/assets for mention
5. **Track:** Monitor citations and traffic

**For Free/Organic Mentions:**
1. **HARO:** Sign up for Help a Reporter Out
   - Respond to relevant queries
   - Provide expert quotes
   - Build relationships with journalists

2. **Product Reviews:**
   - Submit product to G2, Capterra, GetApp
   - Encourage customers to leave reviews
   - Respond to reviews authentically

3. **Expert Positioning:**
   - Position yourself as expert in your space
   - Offer to be quoted in articles
   - Provide data/insights journalists can use

4. **Comparison Pages:**
   - Create accurate comparison content
   - Submit to G2, TechRadar comparison sections
   - Update regularly as market evolves

---

#### Step 4: Tracking & Measurement

**What to Track:**
- Which affiliate sites mention you
- Which prompts lead to affiliate citations
- Traffic from affiliate sites
- Conversion rates from affiliate traffic

**Tools:**
- **Google Analytics:** Track referral traffic
- **ChatGPT/Perplexity:** Test prompts to see citations
- **Mention tracking:** Use tools like Mention, Brand24

---

- [ ] Identified target affiliate sites (B2B vs B2C)
- [ ] Researched partnership options (paid vs free)
- [ ] Created pitch deck (if going paid route)
- [ ] Signed up for HARO (if going free route)
- [ ] Submitted product for review (G2, Capterra, etc.)
- [ ] Created comparison content

## 4. PR & Media Mentions Strategy

### Why It Works
- **Ethan Smith:** Industry publications, thought leadership placement
- **Nick Lafferty:** Off-property content is more authoritative
- **Key insight:** Multiple citation sources increase AI visibility

### Step-by-Step Implementation

#### Step 1: Identify Target Publications

**B2B SaaS Publications:**
- **TechCrunch** - Startup/tech news
- **VentureBeat** - Tech business news
- **The Information** - Tech industry insights
- **Protocol** - Tech policy/business
- **Industry-specific:** Find publications in your specific industry

**General Business Publications:**
- **Forbes** - Business/entrepreneurship
- **Inc.** - Small business/startups
- **Fast Company** - Innovation/business
- **Harvard Business Review** - Business strategy

**Your Industry:** Identify publications your customers read

---

#### Step 2: PR Approaches

**Option A: Press Releases**
- **When:** Product launches, major updates, funding, partnerships
- **How:** Use PR distribution services (PR Newswire, Business Wire)
- **Cost:** $200-$1,000+ per release
- **Best for:** Major announcements

**Option B: Media Outreach**
- **What it is:** Pitch stories directly to journalists
- **How it works:**
  - Research journalists covering your space
  - Create personalized pitch
  - Follow up (but don't spam)
- **Cost:** Time
- **Best for:** Ongoing PR strategy

**Option C: Thought Leadership**
- **What it is:** Position yourself as expert through content
- **How it works:**
  - Write guest posts for publications
  - Offer expert quotes for articles
  - Speak at industry events
  - Create data/insights journalists want
- **Cost:** Time
- **Best for:** Building authority

**Option D: HARO (Help a Reporter Out)**
- **What it is:** Respond to journalist queries
- **How it works:**
  - Sign up for HARO
  - Get daily emails with journalist queries
  - Respond with expert quotes
  - Build relationships with journalists
- **Cost:** Free (time to respond)
- **Best for:** Getting started with PR

---

#### Step 3: Implementation Steps

**For Press Releases:**
1. **Identify newsworthy events:** Launches, updates, milestones
2. **Write press release:** Follow standard format
3. **Distribute:** Use PR distribution service or direct outreach
4. **Follow up:** Contact journalists directly for important stories
5. **Track:** Monitor mentions and citations

**For Media Outreach:**
1. **Research journalists:** Find writers covering your space
2. **Create pitch:** Personalized, newsworthy angle
3. **Outreach:** Email journalists (personalized, not mass email)
4. **Follow up:** Once, after 1 week if no response
5. **Build relationships:** Long-term relationship building

**For Thought Leadership:**
1. **Identify topics:** What can you speak authoritatively about?
2. **Create content:** Articles, data, insights
3. **Pitch publications:** Guest post opportunities
4. **Speak at events:** Industry conferences, webinars
5. **Provide quotes:** Respond to journalist queries

**For HARO:**
1. **Sign up:** HelpAReporter.com
2. **Set up alerts:** Get daily emails with queries
3. **Respond quickly:** Journalists need fast responses
4. **Provide value:** Expert quotes, data, insights
5. **Build relationships:** Follow up with journalists you've helped

---

#### Step 4: Tracking & Measurement

**What to Track:**
- Media mentions (which publications)
- Which prompts lead to media citations
- Traffic from media mentions
- Brand awareness metrics

**Tools:**
- **Google Alerts:** Track brand mentions
- **Mention/Brand24:** Media monitoring
- **ChatGPT/Perplexity:** Test prompts for citations
- **Google Analytics:** Track referral traffic

---

- [ ] Researched journalists covering your space

## 5. Off-Property Content Strategy

- **Key insight:** Partner network of 5-10 sites is scalable

### Step-by-Step Implementation

#### Step 1: Identify Partner Sites

**Types of Partner Sites:**
- **Guest post opportunities:** Industry blogs that accept guest posts
- **Partner companies:** Companies you work with (co-marketing)
- **Industry publications:** Publications accepting contributed content
- **Community sites:** Industry forums, communities

**How to Find:**
- Research competitors: Where do they publish?
- Google search: "[your industry] guest post" or "write for us"
- Industry associations: Member publications
- Partner companies: Co-marketing opportunities

---

#### Step 2: Content Strategy

**Content Types:**
1. **Guest posts:** Write articles for partner sites
2. **Co-marketing:** Create content with partners

4. **Case studies:** Share case studies on partner sites

**Content Topics:**
- Answer target prompts (same as YouTube strategy)
- Comparison content (especially effective)
- How-to guides
- Industry insights

#### Step 3: Implementation Steps

1. **Research partners:** Identify 5-10 target sites
2. **Reach out:** Pitch guest post or co-marketing

## 📊 Overall Citation Strategy Tracking

### Visibility Tracking (Offense/Defense)

**Offense Strategy (Low Visibility Gaps):**
- Track where you have low visibility
- Identify prompts where competitors are cited, you're not
- Create content to fill gaps
- Focus on high-value prompts

**Defense Strategy (High Visibility Maintenance):**
- Track where you have high visibility
- Protect cited pages (don't break them)
- Maintain content quality
- Update regularly

**Tracking Setup:**
1. **Create prompt list:** 50-100 target prompts
2. **Test regularly:** Ask ChatGPT/Perplexity each prompt
3. **Track citations:** Are you cited? Which sources?
4. **Calculate visibility score:** "Visible in X out of 10 prompts"
5. **Identify gaps:** Where are you not visible?
6. **Create content:** Fill gaps with targeted content

**Tools:**
- **Spreadsheet:** Track prompts, citations, visibility scores
- **ChatGPT/Perplexity:** Test prompts manually
- **Automation:** Tools like Profound (if budget allows)

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Spam:** Don't spam Reddit or other platforms (gets banned)
2. **Low quality:** Don't create low-quality content just for volume
3. **Ignoring tracking:** Track what works, don't guess
4. **Giving up too early:** Results can take 2-4 weeks
5. **Not being authentic:** Transparency builds trust
6. **Over-focusing on one channel:** Diversify citation sources
