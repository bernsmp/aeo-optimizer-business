## 📊 Critical Stats to Remember

### Video Dominance
- **3.1x** more likely to be cited than text
- **29.5%** of Google AI Overview citations from YouTube
- **78%** of product comparison queries include video
- **200x** YouTube advantage over Vimeo

### Review Platform Power
- **33-75%** of review citations go to G2 (platform-dependent)
- Companies in **5+ authority sources** = **2.7x** higher mention rates
- G2 = **#2 overall** most-cited domain

### Reddit Supremacy
- **#1** most-cited domain overall
- **81%** of technical/B2B queries cite Reddit
- **3.8x** higher citation odds for niche subreddits
- Average cited post = **1.5 years old** (evergreen)

### The Virality Paradox
- Cited posts have **67% fewer upvotes**
- Cited posts have **61% fewer comments**
- 300-800 word substantive answers perform best
- AI seeks definitive answers, not viral debates

### SEO-AEO Overlap Crisis
- Only **8-12%** overlap between ChatGPT citations and Google top 10
- Product comparison queries = **negative correlation** (r ≈ -0.98)
- Need parallel optimization strategies

### Platform Overlap
- Only **12%** overlap between different AI platforms
- Must optimize for each platform separately

### Technical Barriers
- **68%** of enterprise SaaS sites block AI crawlers
- Fixing access = first mentions **within days**
- **40%** of target queries within **2 weeks** after fixing

### Structured Data Multiplier
- **3x** more likely to be cited with proper schema markup
- Critical schemas: FAQ, HowTo, Product, VideoObject, Article

### Company Blog Reality
- **<5%** direct citation rate overall
- **17%** for B2B-specific queries (5-6x increase)
- Brand-owned content "completely non-existent" in top 10

### Security/Executive Framing
- **2-3x** more citations with security/compliance framing
- Executive decision-maker content significantly outperforms feature descriptions

## 📁 Content Type Performance

### By Category
1. **Social & UGC:** 25-35% (Reddit, G2, forums, LinkedIn)
2. **Affiliates & Listicles:** 20-30% (PCMag, Capterra, TechRadar)
3. **Video Content:** 15-30% (platform-dependent)
4. **News & Publishers:** 15-25% (Forbes, Reuters, CIO.com)
5. **Company Blogs:** <5% direct, 17% for B2B queries
6. **Industry Reports:** 5-10% (Gartner, analyst firms)

### By Format
1. **Structured Comparisons:** 35-45% of B2B SaaS citations
2. **Discussion Threads & Q&A:** 25-35%
3. **Tutorial & How-To:** 20-30% (video 47% citation rate)
4. **News & Trend Analysis:** 10-20%
5. **Product Marketing:** <5%

### High-Citation Content Characteristics
- ✅ Question-based titles (+63% higher scores)
- ✅ Structured formatting (H2/H3, bullets, tables)
- ✅ 300-800 word substantive answers (Reddit sweet spot)
- ✅ 10-20 minute videos (3.4x > <5 min videos)
- ✅ Specific technical details, metrics, timelines
- ✅ Balanced pros/cons (5% positive = 6.1% negative both cited)
- ✅ FAQ schema and structured data
- ✅ Evergreen content (avg 1.5 years old)

### Low-Citation Content
- ❌ Marketing jargon and promotional language
- ❌ Viral content (high engagement, low signal)
- ❌ Breaking news without context
- ❌ Very short or extremely long content
- ❌ Generic titles without specific problem framing
- ❌ Gated or paywalled content
- ❌ Pure opinion without rationale

## 💡 Strategic Insights

### The Third-Party Validation Imperative
- Brand content "completely non-existent" in top 10
- Success = (1) Brand creates content → (2) Third-party references it → (3) AI cites third-party
- Review platforms cite brands **4-10x more** than brand sites

### The Zero-Click Paradigm
- Traditional SEO = drive clicks
- AEO = AI cites brand without user clicking
- Influence at discovery stage before visiting any website
- Metrics change: "traffic" → "citations" + "share of voice"

### Platform-Specific Behaviors
- **ChatGPT:** High concentration (top 20 = 67.3%)
- **Perplexity:** Broad distribution (top 20 = 28.5%)
- Only 12% overlap between platforms
- Need distinct strategies for each

### Niche Subreddit Authority Effect
- Specialized subreddits outperform large general communities
- Query-specific subreddits = 3.8x higher odds
- Better to dominate 3-5 relevant than spread across 20

### Security and Executive Framing Multiplier
- Security/compliance framing = 2-3x more citations
- CIO.com dominates B2B citations
- Executives control budgets, AI knows this

## 🎬 Video Optimization Checklist

- [ ] **Length:** 10-20 minutes (sweet spot)
- [ ] **Title:** Question-based, conversational, includes keyword
- [ ] **Description:** 200-300 words, front-load keywords
- [ ] **Transcript:** Accurate, timestamped, topic sections
- [ ] **Chapters:** Allow AI to cite specific moments
- [ ] **Tags:** Mix broad and specific, entity-based
- [ ] **Quality:** Clear audio, professional presentation
- [ ] **Structure:** Answer in first 2 min, then expand
- [ ] **Host:** YouTube (mandatory), not Vimeo
- [ ] **Embed:** On website with 500+ word text
- [ ] **Schema:** VideoObject structured data
- [ ] **Cross-post:** LinkedIn with 200-300 word context

- [ ] **Ratio:** 90/10 helpful-to-promotional
