## Key Validation Points: What We Got Right

**Your existing strategy:** YouTube/Vimeo is already your #1 priority in the citation guide

**Action:** Continue aggressive video content production. The research validates this is THE highest-ROI activity.

### ✅ Reddit Authentic Engagement (Already Your #2 Priority)

**Research confirms:**
- Reddit is the **#1 most-cited domain overall**
- **81% of technical/B2B queries** on ChatGPT cite Reddit
- Niche subreddits have **3.8x higher citation odds** than general communities
- Average cited post is **1.5 years old** (evergreen compounds)

**Your existing strategy:** Reddit authentic engagement is already your #2 priority

**Action:** Stay the course. Focus on 3-5 niche AEO/SEO subreddits with 90/10 helpful-to-promotional ratio.

### ✅ The "Virality Paradox" We Discovered

**Research confirms:**
- Cited Reddit posts have **67% fewer upvotes** and **61% fewer comments**
- AI seeks definitive answers, not viral debates
- 300-800 word substantive answers in focused threads perform best

**Your existing strategy:** Already emphasizes helpful, detailed answers over promotional content

**Action:** This validates your approach. Focus on technical depth, not social engagement metrics.

### 🆕 4. Platform-Specific Citation Behaviors (Critical Differentiation)

**Research findings:**
- Only **8-12% overlap** between ChatGPT citations and Google rankings
- Only **12% overlap** between AI platforms
- Each platform has distinct source preferences requiring tailored approaches

**Gap in current strategy:** Treats all AI platforms similarly

**Action for mAIX:**

#### **ChatGPT Strategy:**
- Favors encyclopedic authority (Wikipedia 27%, Reddit ~7%)
- Focus: Build citation-worthy original research → Media cites it → Wikipedia references media
- Content: Comprehensive encyclopedic guides, factual verified information
- Target: .org domains and institutional backing

#### **Perplexity Strategy:**
- Favors expert curation (38% blogs/editorial, Reddit 6.6%, G2 75% of reviews)
- Focus: Reddit + LinkedIn + G2 combination (Perplexity uniquely values all three)
- Content: Expert buying guides, community peer experiences, diverse perspectives
- Target: Broad relationships across mid-tier specialist outlets

#### **Google AI Overviews Strategy:**
- Favors engagement signals (Reddit dominant, YouTube 29.5%)
- Focus: Video + blog hybrid with high engagement indicators
- Content: YouTube tutorials embedded in blog posts with engagement metrics
- Target: Deep nested pages with community validation

#### **Gemini Strategy:**
- Favors multimodal content (YouTube #1, structured data emphasis)
- Focus: Video + blog + structured data trifecta
- Content: Multimodal assets with machine-readable metadata
- Target: Clean entity definitions and relationships

**Update to citation guide:** Add platform-specific optimization checklists for each major AI engine

**Action for mAIX:**

   - Whitelist AI crawlers in WAF configs

2. **Add to mAIX service offering:**
   - Include "AI Crawler Audit" as first diagnostic step for all clients
   - Create automated tool to check client websites for crawler blocking

   - Position as "quick win" that delivers results in days

**Expected impact:** Immediate visibility improvements within days for clients with access issues

## Measurement Framework for mAIX Clients

### **Primary KPIs (Zero-Click Success)**
1. **Citation frequency** across major AI platforms
   - ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Copilot
   - Track monthly with Profound, Passionfruit, or manual monitoring

2. **Share of voice** in category queries
   - % of target queries where client is cited
   - Competitive comparison vs. top 3 competitors

3. **Citation position and prominence**
   - First citation vs. buried in response
   - Positive, neutral, or negative framing

### **Secondary KPIs (Business Impact)**
4. **AI-referred conversions**
   - Track referrer source (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.)
   - Measure trials, demos, purchases from AI traffic

5. **Branded search volume**
   - Monitor spikes after AI visibility improvements
   - Indirect signal of AI discovery

6. **Pipeline influence**
   - Customer surveys: "How did you first hear about us?"
   - Track "ChatGPT/AI search" as discovery method

7. **CAC reduction**
   - Compare cost per acquisition for AI-referred leads
   - Typical reduction: 24-31% from organic AI traffic

### **Supporting Metrics**
8. **Review platform performance**
   - Review count vs. top 3 competitors
   - Average rating maintenance (4.0+ minimum)
   - Category ranking on G2/Capterra

9. **Reddit community metrics**
   - Authentic brand mentions per month
   - Sentiment distribution
   - Subreddit authority score

10. **Video performance**
    - Video views and watch time
    - Citation frequency per video
    - Video-driven website traffic

11. **Media coverage**
    - Placements per quarter
    - Citation conversion rate (% that generate AI citations)
    - Cross-platform presence (# of platforms citing each article)
