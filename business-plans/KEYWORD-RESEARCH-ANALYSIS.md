# Keyword Research Analysis Guide

## What to Analyze

Once your keyword research files are available, I'll analyze:

### 1. Search Volume Analysis
- Which terms have highest volume?
- Which terms have low volume but low competition?
- Best volume/competition ratio

### 2. Term Comparison
- "AEO" vs "AI SEO" vs "ChatGPT SEO"
- "Answer Engine Optimization" vs alternatives
- Related keyword opportunities

### 3. Content Opportunities
- Long-tail keywords to target
- Question keywords (great for FAQs)
- Low competition keywords

### 4. Strategic Recommendations
- Primary term choice
- Secondary terms
- Content strategy based on data
