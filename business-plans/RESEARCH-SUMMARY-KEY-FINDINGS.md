## 🔍 Key Insights We Learned

### 1. Citation Strategy Priority

**Ethan's Priority Order:**
1. **YouTube/Vimeo** - Easy, works well, especially B2B (less competition)
2. **Reddit** - HUGE opportunity, but authentic only (no spam)
3. **Affiliates** - Dotdash/Meredith, Forbes (if budget allows)
4. **Blogs** - Industry publications
5. **Your own site** - Traditional SEO

**Action:** ✅ Prioritize YouTube/Vimeo in Layer 2

---

### 2. Reddit Strategy (Critical!)

**Ethan's Approach:**
- Don't spam (gets banned)
- Be authentic: "This is my name, this is where I work, here's useful information"
- Find threads that are part of citations you want to show up in
- Even 5 comments can work
- Community manages spam well

**Action:** ✅ Add Reddit authentic engagement strategy to Layer 2

---

### 3. Help Center Optimization (Underrated!)

**Ethan's Insight:**
- People ask follow-up questions: "Does your product do this?", "What integrations?"
- Usually on subdomain (bad) - move to subdirectory
- Need cross-linking
- Fill in the tail (obscure use cases)

**Action:** ✅ Add help center optimization to Layer 1

---

### 4. Long-Tail Question Content

**Ethan's Insight:**
- Average query length: 25 words (vs 6 for SEO)
- Much larger tail = more opportunities
- Answer ALL follow-up questions
- More questions answered = better ranking

**Action:** ✅ Emphasize long-tail questions in Layer 1

---

### 5. Head vs Tail Strategy

**Ethan's Key Difference:**
- **Head (high-volume):** Being mentioned MOST OFTEN wins (not ranking #1)
- **Tail (long-tail):** Much larger in chat, early stage can win immediately

**Action:** ✅ Focus Layer 1 on tail, Layer 2 on head citations

**Ethan's Study:**

- **100% AI-generated** = Bad

**Your Advantage:** ✅ You're not selling AI-generated content
