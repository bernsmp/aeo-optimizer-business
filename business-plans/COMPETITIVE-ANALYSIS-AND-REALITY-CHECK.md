# Competitive Reality Check: Will Other AEO Sites Already Exist?

## The Honest Answer

**Yes, probably.** But that doesn't mean you can't win. Here's the realistic assessment and how to compete anyway.

---

## What Likely Already Exists

### 1. **Generic SEO Agencies Claiming "AI SEO"**
- Big agencies (Moz, Ahrefs, SEMrush) probably have some AI-related content
- They're adapting old SEO advice to AI
- **Weakness:** Not AEO-specific, generic advice

### 2. **"AI SEO" Blog Posts**
- Thousands of articles about "optimizing for ChatGPT"
- Mostly surface-level, no real methodology
- **Weakness:** No actual service, just content

### 3. **Schema Markup Services**
- Companies that add structured data
- Part of what AEO needs, but not complete
- **Weakness:** Missing FAQs, AI-specific optimization

### 4. **New "AEO" Companies**
- Someone else probably had this idea
- Might already have a site
- **Weakness:** Unknown quality, might not have your expertise

---

## How to Check What Actually Exists

### Step 1: Search These Terms

**Run these searches and document what you find:**

1. **"AEO" or "Answer Engine Optimization"**
   - How many results?
   - Who's ranking #1?
   - What's the quality of content?

2. **"ChatGPT SEO" or "AI SEO"**
   - Who dominates?
   - What's missing from their content?

3. **"Optimize for Perplexity" or "Perplexity SEO"**
   - Is anyone doing this well?
   - What gaps exist?

4. **"[Your city] AI SEO consultant"**
   - Local competition check
   - Opportunity for local dominance

### Step 2: Analyze the Competition

**For each competitor, ask:**

- ✅ Do they have a real service? (or just content?)
- ✅ What's their pricing? (too high = opportunity)
- ✅ What's missing from their offering?
- ✅ How fast do they deliver?
- ✅ Do they show real results?
- ✅ Are they actually doing the work or just talking about it?

### Step 3: Find Your Differentiation

**Even if 10 sites exist, you can win by:**

1. **Being faster** (24-hour delivery vs 2 weeks)
2. **Being more complete** (full package vs just schemas)
3. **Showing real proof** (case studies vs theory)
4. **Being cheaper** (if they're overpriced)
5. **Being more specific** (industry expertise)
6. **Better content** (deeper, more practical)

## Three Competitive Scenarios & Strategies

### Scenario 1: "AEO" is Wide Open (Best Case)

**What it means:** Few or no sites ranking for "AEO"

**Your strategy:**
- ✅ Own the category immediately
- ✅ Create the "What is AEO?" definitive guide
- ✅ Build backlinks by being THE source
- ✅ Move fast before others catch on

**Timeline to dominance:** 3-6 months

---

### Scenario 2: Some Competition Exists (Most Likely)

**What it means:** 5-20 sites talking about AEO/AI SEO

**Your strategy:**
- ✅ **Don't compete head-on** - find gaps
- ✅ **Be more specific** - "AEO for [industry]" vs generic
- ✅ **Show real results** - case studies they don't have
- ✅ **Faster delivery** - if they take weeks, you take days
- ✅ **Better content** - go deeper than they do

**Example gaps to exploit:**
- They write about AEO → You DO AEO (service)
- They have generic guides → You have industry-specific
- They show theory → You show real client results
- They're expensive → You're accessible
- They're slow → You're fast

**Timeline to relevance:** 6-12 months

---

### Scenario 3: Market is Crowded (Worst Case)

**What it means:** 50+ sites, big players dominate

**Your strategy:**
- ✅ **Niche down hard** - "AEO for [specific industry]"
- ✅ **Local focus** - "[Your city] AEO expert"
- ✅ **Different angle** - "AEO for small businesses" vs enterprise
- ✅ **Better execution** - Do what they do, but better
- ✅ **Community building** - Build audience they don't have

**Example niches:**
- "AEO for Law Firms in [State]"
- "AEO for E-commerce Stores Under $1M Revenue"
- "AEO for Local Service Businesses"
- "AEO for SaaS Companies"

**Timeline to profitability:** 12-18 months (but still profitable)

## How to Compete Even If Others Exist

### Strategy 1: The "Better Mousetrap" Approach

**Don't reinvent the wheel. Just do it better.**

**Example:**
- Competitor: "AEO Guide" (2,000 words, generic)
- You: "AEO Guide" (5,000 words, with real examples, case studies, screenshots)

**How to win:**
- Go 2x deeper than competitors
- Include real examples they don't have
- Update more frequently
- Answer questions they don't

---

### Strategy 2: The "Niche Down" Approach

**Don't compete in "AEO" - compete in "AEO for [specific thing]"**

**Examples:**
- ❌ "AEO Guide" (crowded)
- ✅ "AEO for Real Estate Agents" (less crowded)
- ✅ "AEO for Local Restaurants" (even less crowded)
- ✅ "AEO for [Your City] Businesses" (minimal competition)

**How to win:**
- Own a smaller category completely
- Become THE expert in that niche
- Expand from there

---

### Strategy 3: The "Content + Service" Approach

**Most competitors are EITHER content OR service. You're BOTH.**

**The advantage:**
- Content drives traffic
- Service provides proof
- Proof makes content better
- Better content drives more traffic

**How to win:**
- Content sites can't show real results
- Service sites don't create content
- You do both = unbeatable

---

### Strategy 4: The "Speed" Approach

**If competitors take 2 weeks, you take 24 hours.**

**How to win:**
- Faster delivery = competitive advantage
- Clients choose speed
- Speed becomes your brand
- Hard for competitors to match

---

### Strategy 5: The "Proof" Approach

**Show what competitors can't - real results.**

**How to win:**
- Screenshots of ChatGPT results
- Real client testimonials
- Before/after traffic data
- Case studies with names (with permission)

**Most competitors can't do this because:**
- They don't actually do the work
- They don't have clients
- They're just writing theory

## Realistic Content Strategy (Even With Competition)

### Phase 1: Find the Gaps (Month 1)

**Don't write what exists. Write what's missing.**

**Research process:**
1. Find top 10 articles for "AEO"
2. Read them all
3. List what's missing:
   - Specific examples?
   - Real case studies?
   - Step-by-step guides?
   - Industry-specific advice?
   - Tools/resources?
4. Write about the gaps

**Example:**
- Competitor article: "5 AEO Tips" (generic)
- Your article: "5 AEO Tips That Actually Worked: Real Case Studies from 10 Clients"

---

### Phase 2: Go Deeper (Month 2-3)

**If competitors write 1,000 words, you write 5,000.**

**How to go deeper:**
- Include screenshots
- Add video walkthroughs
- Provide templates
- Share real examples
- Answer edge cases
- Update regularly

**Example:**
- Competitor: "How to Add Schema Markup"
- You: "How to Add Schema Markup: Complete Guide with 47 Examples, Common Mistakes, and Real Results from 20 Clients"

---

### Phase 3: Build Authority (Month 4-6)

**Become THE source by:**

1. **Creating original research**
   - "We Analyzed 500 Websites: Here's What Works"
   - "AEO Score: Ranking 1,000 Businesses"
   - Industry reports

2. **Building community**
   - Email list
   - Facebook group
   - Regular webinars
   - Q&A sessions

3. **Getting backlinks**
   - Guest posts
   - HARO pitches
   - Resource page links
   - Broken link building
