- Enter: "AEO"
- Document:

  - **CPC:** Cost per click (indicates value)
  - **Trend:** Rising/falling/stable

#### 2. "Answer Engine Optimization"
- Same process
- Document all metrics

#### 3. "AI SEO"
- Same process
- Document all metrics

#### 4. "ChatGPT SEO"
- Same process
- Document all metrics

#### 5. "ChatGPT optimization"
- Same process
- Document all metrics

### Step 2: Related Keywords Discovery (10 min)

**Tool:** SEMrush Keyword Magic Tool

**For each term, check:**

#### Search: "AEO"
- Click "Related Keywords"
- Look for:
  - Long-tail variations
  - Question keywords ("what is AEO")
  - Competitor terms
  - Related searches

#### Search: "AI SEO"
- Same process
- Compare volume vs "AEO"

**What to look for:**
- Keywords with volume but low KD
- Question keywords (great for content)
- Long-tail opportunities
- Related terms people actually search

**Document top 10 related keywords for each term**

---

### Step 3: Keyword Difficulty Analysis (10 min)

**Tool:** SEMrush Keyword Difficulty

**For each primary term:**

1. **"AEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - What does it mean?
     - KD 0-30 = Easy (can rank in 3-6 months)
     - KD 31-60 = Medium (can rank in 6-12 months)
     - KD 61-100 = Hard (need strong domain authority)

2. **"AI SEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - Compare to "AEO"

3. **"ChatGPT SEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - Compare to others

**Decision:**
- If "AEO" KD < 30 = Good opportunity
- If "AI SEO" KD > 70 = Very competitive
- Choose term with best volume/KD ratio

### Step 4: Competitor Analysis (15 min)

**Tool:** SEMrush Domain Overview

**For top-ranking sites:**

#### Find Competitors:
1. Google search "AEO" or "AI SEO"
2. Note top 5 ranking sites
3. Enter each domain in SEMrush

**For each competitor, check:**

1. **Domain Authority**
   - What's their authority score?
   - Can you compete?

2. **Top Keywords**
   - What keywords do they rank for?

3. **Traffic**

**Document:**

- Keywords with volume but low competition

## Decision Framework Using SEMrush Data

### Choose Primary Term Based On:

**Best Volume/KD Ratio:**
- High volume + Low KD = Best opportunity
- Medium volume + Low KD = Good opportunity
- High volume + High KD = Competitive but possible
- Low volume + Any KD = Don't use as primary

**Example Decision:**

| Term | Volume | KD | Score | Decision |
|------|--------|----|-------|----------|
| "AEO" | 50 | 15 | Low | Secondary (low volume) |
| "AI SEO" | 8,000 | 75 | High | Competitive but use |
| "ChatGPT SEO" | 2,000 | 45 | Medium | Good opportunity |

**Recommendation:** Use "AI SEO" as primary (proven demand), "ChatGPT SEO" as secondary (growing, less competitive), "AEO" as brand term.
