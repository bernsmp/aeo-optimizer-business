## ✅ New Additions to Layer 1: Technical AEO Foundation

### 1. Comparison Pages (Essential)
- **What:** Create comprehensive comparison pages vs competitors/alternatives
- **Why:** AI frequently handles "X vs Y" queries
- **Implementation:** Detailed feature comparisons, pricing, use cases
- **SEO Benefit:** Captures comparison queries, featured snippets
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents

### 2. Q&A Format Content
- **What:** Structure content in Q&A format
- **Why:** Answer multiple related questions on single pages
- **Implementation:** Use FAQ schema markup, cover all variations
- **SEO Benefit:** FAQ rich results, featured snippets
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents

### 3. Prompt Tracking & Research
- **What:** Track prompts (not just keywords) that lead to citations
- **Why:** Identify which prompts mention your brand
- **Implementation:** Monitor prompt patterns, volume, trends
- **SEO Benefit:** Better content targeting, long-tail coverage
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents

### 4. Content Velocity Strategy
- **What:** Prioritize fast content creation and publishing
- **Why:** AI indexes new content in 2 days (vs weeks for Google)
- **Implementation:** Create content quickly to capture emerging prompts
- **SEO Benefit:** Faster indexing, early mover advantage
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents
- **Messaging:** "Velocity advantage: Fast content = fast results"

## ✅ New Additions to Layer 2: Visibility Strategy

### 1. Off-Property Content Strategy
- **What:** Create content on partner sites, affiliates, guest posts
- **Why:** Multiple citation sources increase AI visibility
- **Implementation:** Build presence across multiple authoritative domains
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents

### 2. Visibility Tracking (Offense/Defense)
- **What:** Data-driven visibility strategy
- **Offense Strategy:** Track where you have low visibility (gaps to fill)
- **Defense Strategy:** Track where you have high visibility (maintain)
- **Implementation:** Monitor "share of voice" across LLMs, track by prompt category
- **Status:** ✅ Added to all framework documents
- **Messaging:** "Data-driven visibility strategy"
