# GEO vs AEO: Are They The Same or Different?

## Quick Answer

**They're essentially the same thing, but with slight nuance:**

- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** = Broader term, optimizing for all generative AI search engines
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** = More specific term, focused on getting cited as direct answers

**In practice:** Most people use them interchangeably. You can use both terms strategically.

---

## The Technical Difference

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
**Definition:** Optimizing content to appear in AI-powered generative search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, etc.)

**Focus:** 
- All generative AI platforms
- Broader optimization approach
- Multiple AI systems

**Coined by:** Researchers/academics studying AI search

---

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
**Definition:** Optimizing content to be cited as direct answers in AI responses

**Focus:**
- Getting cited/quoted in AI answers
- Direct answer format
- Answer-focused optimization

**Coined by:** Marketing/SEO community

---

## Are They Actually Different?

### Short Answer: **Not Really**

Both terms describe the same practice:
- Optimizing for AI search engines
- Getting content cited in AI responses
- Technical implementation (schemas, robots.txt, llms.txt)
- Brand visibility strategy

**The difference is mostly semantic** - GEO is the academic term, AEO is the marketing term.

---

## Which Term Should You Use?

### Use Both Strategically:

**1. Use "GEO" When:**
- Referencing academic research
- Talking to technical audiences
- Discussing the broader concept
- Mentioning "Generative Engine Optimization" as the full term

**2. Use "AEO" When:**
- Marketing to businesses
- SEO/marketing contexts
- Answer-focused messaging
- More memorable/acronym-friendly

**3. Use Both Together:**
- "GEO/AEO" or "AEO (also called GEO)"
- Shows you understand both terms
- Captures both audiences
- SEO benefit (both keywords)
