### Step 3: Keyword Difficulty Analysis (10 min)

**Tool:** SEMDash Keyword Difficulty / Competition Analysis

**For each primary term:**

1. **"AEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - What does it mean?
     - KD 0-30 = Easy (can rank in 3-6 months)
     - KD 31-60 = Medium (can rank in 6-12 months)
     - KD 61-100 = Hard (need strong domain authority)

2. **"AI SEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - Compare to "AEO"

3. **"ChatGPT SEO"**
   - KD Score: ___________
   - Compare to others

**Also check:**
- SERP features (Featured snippets, People Also Ask)
- Top ranking pages (domain authority)
- Content type ranking (blogs vs product pages)

**Decision:**
- If "AEO" KD < 30 = Good opportunity
- If "AI SEO" KD > 70 = Very competitive
- Choose term with best volume/KD ratio

## Decision Framework Using SEMDash Data

### Choose Primary Term Based On:

**Best Volume/KD Ratio:**
- High volume + Low KD = Best opportunity
- Medium volume + Low KD = Good opportunity
- High volume + High KD = Competitive but possible
- Low volume + Any KD = Don't use as primary

**Example Decision:**

| Term | Volume | KD | Score | Decision |
|------|--------|----|-------|----------|
| "AEO" | 50 | 15 | Low | Secondary (low volume) |
| "AI SEO" | 8,000 | 75 | High | Competitive but use |
| "ChatGPT SEO" | 2,000 | 45 | Medium | Good opportunity |

**Recommendation:** Use "AI SEO" as primary (proven demand), "ChatGPT SEO" as secondary (growing, less competitive), "AEO" as brand term.
