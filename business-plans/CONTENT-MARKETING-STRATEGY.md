## How to Stand Out in a Crowded Space

### 1. **Be Specific, Not Generic**

❌ Bad: "How to Use AI for SEO"
✅ Good: "How to Make ChatGPT Recommend Your Law Firm Instead of Competitors"

❌ Bad: "AI SEO Tips"
✅ Good: "The Exact Schema Markup That Made a Client Rank #1 in Perplexity"

**Specificity = Less competition = Higher rankings**

---

### 2. **Show Real Results**

Every article should include:
- Actual screenshots
- Real client results (with permission)
- Before/after comparisons
- Specific numbers

**Example:**
- "How We Got a Client 47 ChatGPT Recommendations in 30 Days"
- Include screenshots of ChatGPT results
- Show the exact changes made
- Share the client's traffic increase

---

### 3. **Create Original Research**

**Ideas:**
- "We Analyzed 1,000 Websites: Here's What Makes Them Visible to AI"
- "The AEO Score: Ranking 500 Businesses by AI Visibility"
- "Industry Report: Which Sectors Are Winning at AI Search?"

**Original data = Backlinks = Authority**

---

### 4. **Solve Real Problems**

Don't write "10 AI SEO Tips." Write:
- "Why ChatGPT Can't Find Your Business (And the 3 Exact Fixes)"
- "The Schema Error That's Making You Invisible to AI"
- "How to Fix Your FAQ Page So AI Actually Uses It"

**Problem-solving content ranks better than generic advice**

---

### 5. **Use Your Service as Content**

Every client = potential case study:
- "AEO Case Study: [Client Name]"
- "How We Fixed [Specific Problem] for [Industry]"
- "Before/After: [Client] AI Visibility Transformation"

**This creates:**
- Social proof
- Real examples
- SEO value
- Lead generation
