## How to Use "AEO" in Content

### ✅ DO:
- "AI SEO (we call it AEO) is..."
- "Our AEO method for AI SEO..."
- "Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is..."
- Use as brand/method name

### ❌ DON'T:
- Optimize for "AEO" as primary keyword
- Use "AEO" in title tags (use "AI SEO" instead)
- Expect traffic from "AEO" searches

---

## Content Template

### Title Format:
**"[Primary Keyword]: [Value Prop] (AEO)"**

Examples:
- "AI SEO: The Complete Guide to Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)"
- "AI SEO Services: Complete AEO Optimization"
- "ChatGPT SEO: How to Optimize Using the AEO Method"

### Content Structure:
1. **Hook:** Address the search query directly
2. **Definition:** Explain the term, mention AEO naturally
3. **Value:** Provide comprehensive information
4. **CTA:** Link to your AEO service
