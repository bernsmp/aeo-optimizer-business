## Key Differences & Nuances

### 1. Head vs Tail Strategy

**Ethan's Insight:**
- **Head (high-volume):** Being mentioned MOST OFTEN wins (not ranking #1)
- **Tail (long-tail):** Much larger in chat (25 words vs 6 words)
- Early stage can win the tail immediately

**Your Framework Implication:**
- Layer 1: Focus on long-tail questions (tail strategy)
- Layer 2: Citation optimization for head queries
- Layer 3: Brand strength helps both head and tail

---

### 2. Citation Strategy

**Ethan's Priority Order:**
1. **YouTube/Vimeo** - Easy, works well, especially B2B
2. **Reddit** - HUGE opportunity, but authentic only
3. **Affiliates** - Dotdash/Meredith, Forbes (if budget)
4. **Blogs** - Industry publications
5. **Your own site** - Traditional SEO

**Your Framework:**
- Layer 2 (Visibility) should prioritize:
  1. YouTube/Vimeo videos (especially for B2B)
  2. Reddit authentic engagement
  3. PR/media mentions
  4. Affiliate partnerships

**Update Needed:** ✅ Prioritize YouTube/Vimeo in Layer 2

---

### 3. Reddit Strategy (Critical!)

**Ethan's Approach:**
- Don't spam (gets banned)
- Be authentic: "This is my name, this is where I work, here's useful information"
- Find threads that are part of citations
- Even 5 comments can work
- Community manages spam well

**Your Framework:**
- Layer 2 should include specific Reddit strategy
- Authentic engagement, not spam
- Community-first approach

**Update Needed:** ✅ Add Reddit strategy to Layer 2

---

### 4. Help Center Optimization

**Ethan's Insight:**
- Underrated opportunity
- People ask follow-up questions: "Does your product do this?", "What integrations?"
- Usually on subdomain (bad) - move to subdirectory
- Need cross-linking
- Fill in the tail (obscure use cases)

**Your Framework:**
- Layer 1 should include help center optimization
- Technical foundation includes help center structure

**Update Needed:** ✅ Add help center optimization to Layer 1

---

### 5. Long-Tail Question Content

**Ethan's Insight:**
- Average query length: 25 words (vs 6 for SEO)
- Much larger tail = more opportunities
- Answer ALL follow-up questions
- More questions answered = better ranking

**Your Framework:**
- Layer 1 should emphasize long-tail question content
- Content strategy should focus on follow-up questions

**Update Needed:** ✅ Emphasize long-tail in Layer 1

## What Doesn't Work (Industry Consensus)

### ❌ AI-Generated Content

**Ethan's Study:**
- 10-12% of content in Google/ChatGPT is AI-generated
- 90% is human-written
- AI-generated doesn't rank well
- **AI-assisted** (human in loop) = Good
- **100% AI-generated** = Bad

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Correct - you're not selling AI-generated content
- ✅ You're selling human-created content with technical optimization

---

### ❌ Spam Strategies

**Ethan's Warning:**
- Reddit spam gets banned
- ChatGPT will detect and stop spam
- Same as SEO - spam doesn't work long-term

**Section's Warning:**
- GEO platforms selling "hacks" are struggling
- Shortcuts don't work long-term

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Correct - "We don't sell GEO hacks"
- ✅ Focus on fundamentals, not shortcuts

---

### ❌ Misinformation

**Ethan's Warning:**
- "There's significant misinformation on AEO"
- "Google is dying" = Not true
- "Google's slice of the pie stays the same. The pie gets bigger."
- Don't believe everything you read online

**Your Framework:**
- ✅ Correct - honest positioning
- ✅ Not selling fear ("Google is dying")
- ✅ Selling opportunity ("Pie gets bigger")
