# AEO Reality Check: Insights from Section & Lorelight

## 🎯 Key Takeaway

**"GEO/AEO isn't a hoax, but it isn't a strategy either. It's a reflection of what the world already believes about your brand."**

---

## What This Means for Your AEO Business

### The Good News ✅

1. **There IS Demand**
   - Lorelight had paying customers
   - People want to appear in AI responses
   - The problem isn't demand, it's delivery

2. **Technical Implementation Still Matters**
   - Schema markup, robots.txt, llms.txt = Real value
   - These are concrete, actionable improvements
   - Not "hacks" - these are foundational

3. **You're Not Selling "GEO Hacks"**
   - Your service is technical implementation
   - You're doing the work, not promising magic bullets
   - This is different from GEO strategy platforms

---

### The Challenging News ⚠️

1. **GEO/AEO Tools Are Struggling**
   - Lorelight shut down despite having customers
   - Reason: No long-term strategy, just "hacks"
   - Customers churned because results weren't predictable

2. **The "Magic Bullet" Problem**
   - Companies expect: "Do this one thing, rank #1 in ChatGPT"
   - Reality: "Keep doing the hard work, then you'll show up"
   - Your service needs to set realistic expectations

3. **Non-Deterministic Nature**
   - AI results are personalized
   - Can't reproduce another user's output
   - Hard to prove ROI/effectiveness
