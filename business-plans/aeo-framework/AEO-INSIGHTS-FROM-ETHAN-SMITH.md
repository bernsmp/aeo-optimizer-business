### 3. The Key Differences (Head vs Tail)

**The Head (High-Volume Queries):**
- **SEO:** Ranking #1 = You win
- **AEO:** Being mentioned MOST OFTEN across citations = You win
- **Example:** "What's the best website builder?"
  - In Google: Webflow ranks #1 = they win
  - In ChatGPT: Webflow needs to be mentioned most often across YouTube, Reddit, blogs, affiliates

**The Tail (Long-Tail Queries):**
- **SEO:** Average query = 6 words
- **AEO:** Average query = 25 words
- **Much larger tail** = More opportunities
- **Early stage companies CAN win** (unlike SEO which requires domain authority)

### 6. Citation Strategy (Your Layer 2)

**What Works:**
1. **YouTube/Vimeo Videos** - Easy, no community moderation
2. **Reddit** - HUGE opportunity, but spam doesn't work
3. **Affiliates** - Dotdash/Meredith (Good Housekeeping, AllRecipes, Investopedia)
4. **Blogs** - Industry publications
5. **Your Own Site** - Traditional SEO content

**Reddit Strategy:**
- Don't spam (gets banned)
- Be authentic: "This is my name, this is where I work, here's useful information"
- Even 5 comments can be great
- Community manages spam well

---

### 7. Help Center Optimization (Underrated!)

**Ethan's Insight:**
- People ask follow-up questions: "Does your product do this?", "What are the use cases?", "What integrations?"
- Help center is perfect for answering these
- Usually on subdomain (bad) - move to subdirectory
- Need cross-linking
- Fill in the tail (obscure use cases)

**Add to Your Framework:** Help center optimization as part of Technical Foundation!

---

### 8. What Doesn't Work

**AI-Generated Content:**
- Ethan did rigorous study: 10-12% of content in Google/ChatGPT is AI-generated
- 90% is human-written
- AI-generated doesn't rank well
- **AI-assisted** (human in loop) = Good
- **100% AI-generated** = Bad

**Spam:**
- Reddit spam gets banned
- ChatGPT will detect and stop spam
- Same as SEO - spam doesn't work long-term

---

### 9. Misinformation Problem

**Ethan's Warning:**

- "Google's slice of the pie stays the same. The pie gets bigger."
- Don't believe everything you read online

### 10. Tracking & Measurement

**Answer Tracking:**
