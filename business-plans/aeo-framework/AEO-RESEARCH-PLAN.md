# AEO Research Plan: Validate the Market Before Building

## The Critical Question

**Before building a business around "AEO", you need to know:**
1. Do people search for it?
2. What do they actually search instead?
3. Is there real demand?
4. Who's already doing this?

---

### Step 2: Google Trends Comparison (10 min)

**Tool:** [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com)

**Compare these groups:**

**Group 1:** "AEO" vs "AI SEO" vs "ChatGPT SEO"

---

### Step 5: Content Gap Analysis (30 min)

**Analyze top 10 results for each term:**

**For each top-ranking article, check:**
- [ ] Word count (comprehensive or thin?)
- [ ] Has real examples? (screenshots, case studies)
- [ ] Recent? (2024 or older?)
- [ ] Actionable? (step-by-step or theory?)
- [ ] Has FAQs? (structured for AI citation)

**Document gaps:**
- What's missing from top results?
- What can you do better?
- What questions aren't answered?

**Example gaps to look for:**
- No real case studies
- No screenshots
- Generic advice
- Outdated (pre-2024)
- No industry-specific content
- No tools/resources

## Research Phase 3: Market Demand Validation (1 hour)

### Step 6: Social Media Research (20 min)

**Reddit:**
- Search r/SEO for "AEO", "AI SEO", "ChatGPT SEO"
- Count mentions of each term
- See what questions people ask
- Note engagement levels

**LinkedIn:**
- Search each term
- Count posts using each term
- Check engagement (likes, comments)
- See who's talking about it

**Twitter/X:**
- Search each term
- Check recent mentions
- See hashtag usage
- Note who's influential

**Document:**
- Which term do people actually use?
- Which gets more engagement?
- What questions do they ask?

- Ask: "What is AEO?"

- Search: "AEO"

## What Success Looks Like

### Good Signs:
✅ "AI SEO" has 5,000+ monthly searches
✅ Low competition for specific terms
✅ Content gaps exist (no real examples)
✅ People asking questions on Reddit/LinkedIn
✅ Services exist but are expensive/slow
✅ AI doesn't cite good sources

### Bad Signs:
❌ "AEO" has <50 monthly searches
❌ "AI SEO" is dominated by big players
❌ No questions being asked
❌ No services exist (no demand)
❌ AI already cites good sources

## Next Steps After Research

1. **If "AEO" has no search volume:**
   - Use "AI SEO" as primary term
   - Use "AEO" as brand/method name
   - Build "AEO" category over time

2. **If "AI SEO" is too competitive:**
   - Niche down ("AI SEO for [industry]")
   - Use "ChatGPT SEO" if growing
   - Focus on local/niche

3. **If market is validated:**
   - Create content targeting right terms
   - Position service based on gaps
   - Start building authority
