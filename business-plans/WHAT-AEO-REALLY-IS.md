# What AEO Really Is: The Complete Picture

## 🎯 What AEO Really Is

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** is the practice of making your business visible and citable in AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other LLM-based answer engines.

### The Core Truth About AEO

**AEO is NOT a completely different discipline from SEO.** As Ethan Smith (18 years SEO experience, leading AEO expert) puts it:

> *"Everything that works in SEO works in AEO. But there are additional things beyond SEO that also work in AEO."*

This is the foundational insight that shapes everything else.

---

## 🔑 What You Should Focus On

Based on industry research from experts like Ethan Smith (Graphite) and Nick Lafferty (Profound), here are the **critical focus areas**:

### 1. **Technical Foundation First** (The Infrastructure)

**Why it matters:** AI needs to be able to crawl, understand, and cite your content. Without proper technical setup, you're invisible.

**Focus on:**
- **Schema markup** (Organization, Service, FAQ, Product)
- **AI crawler optimization** (robots.txt, llms.txt for GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, CCBot, PerplexityBot)
- **Content structure** (clear headings, semantic HTML, structured data)
- **Help center optimization** (move from subdomain to subdirectory, cross-linking, fill the tail)
- **Long-tail question content** (25-word queries vs 6-word SEO queries)
- **Comparison pages** (essential - AI frequently handles "X vs Y" queries)
- **Q&A format content** (answer ALL follow-up questions on each page)

**Key insight:** This technical work helps BOTH AEO AND SEO. No wasted effort.

---

### 2. **Citation Strategy** (The Visibility Layer)

**Why it matters:** AI cites what the world already knows. If you're not mentioned anywhere, AI won't cite you.

**Priority order (from Ethan Smith):**
1. **YouTube/Vimeo videos** - Easy to create, heavily cited, especially effective for B2B (less competition)
2. **Reddit authentic engagement** - HUGE opportunity, but spam gets banned. Strategy: Find threads that are part of citations you want, say who you are, where you work, give useful information. Even 5 authentic comments can work.
3. **Affiliate partnerships** - Dotdash/Meredith (Good Housekeeping, AllRecipes, Investopedia), Forbes, TechRadar (for B2B)
4. **Industry publications** - PR, media mentions, thought leadership
5. **Your own site** - Traditional SEO content

**Key insight:** The head (high-volume queries) works differently than SEO. In Google, ranking #1 wins. In AEO, being mentioned MOST OFTEN across citations wins.

---

### 3. **Content Velocity** (The Speed Advantage)

**Why it matters:** ChatGPT can pick up new content within 2 days (vs weeks/months for Google). This is a massive advantage for early-stage companies.

**Focus on:**
- Fast content creation and publishing
- Capturing emerging prompts quickly
- Long-tail questions (25-word queries) where early stage can win immediately
- Comparison pages (create them fast - AI will cite competitors if you don't)

**Key insight:** Early-stage companies CAN win at AEO immediately, unlike SEO which requires domain authority.

---

### 4. **Prompt Tracking** (Not Keyword Tracking)

**Why it matters:** Average prompt is 20+ words (vs 3-5 words for Google). You need to track prompts, not keywords.

**Focus on:**
- Track which prompts lead to citations
- Identify which prompts mention your brand
- Research prompt patterns and variations
- Use prompt insights to guide content creation

**Key insight:** Traffic doesn't matter as much. A Reddit thread from 2 years ago can be cited most. Comparison pages rank higher even without high traffic.

---

### 5. **Brand Strength** (The Real Driver)

**Why it matters:** As Section/Lorelight puts it: *"GEO isn't a strategy, it's a reflection of brand strength."* And Ethan Smith: *"If people don't talk about you, AI won't cite you."*

**Focus on:**
- Building a brand worth talking about
- Creating content that tells your story
- Differentiation that makes you memorable
- Being the company people naturally mention

**Key insight:** This is the real driver of AEO. Technical foundation and citations help, but brand strength determines what gets cited long-term.
