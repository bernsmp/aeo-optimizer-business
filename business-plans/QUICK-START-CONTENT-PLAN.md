## 📝 Content Template: "AEO vs SEO"

### Title:
"AEO vs SEO: What's the Difference? (Complete Guide)"

### Structure:
1. **Quick Answer** (for AEO)
   - "AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on getting content featured as direct answers in AI-powered search, while SEO focuses on ranking web pages in traditional search results."

2. **What is SEO?**
   - Brief explanation
   - Traditional search optimization
   - Goal: Rank higher, get clicks

3. **What is AEO?**
   - Detailed explanation
   - AI-powered search optimization
   - Goal: Be the direct answer

4. **Key Differences** (Table)
   - SEO: Rankings, clicks, keywords
   - AEO: Direct answers, citations, questions

5. **When to Use SEO**
   - Traditional search
   - Website traffic
   - Brand awareness

6. **When to Use AEO**
   - AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity)
   - Voice search
   - Featured snippets

7. **Can You Do Both?**
   - Yes! They complement each other
   - SEO is foundation for AEO
   - Best strategy: Both

8. **How to Implement AEO**
   - Schema markup
   - FAQ sections
   - Structured content
   - **CTA: Use our AEO service**

9. **Conclusion**
   - Summary
   - **CTA: Get AEO audit**
