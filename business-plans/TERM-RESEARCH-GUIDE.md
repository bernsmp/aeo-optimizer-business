# Term Research Guide: Is "AEO" the Dominant Term?

## The Question

**Should you use "AEO" or alternatives like "AI SEO", "ChatGPT SEO", "Answer Engine Optimization"?**

This guide helps you find out which term people actually search for and use.

---

## Terms to Research

### Primary Terms to Check:

1. **"AEO"** or **"Answer Engine Optimization"**
2. **"AI SEO"**
3. **"ChatGPT SEO"** or **"ChatGPT optimization"**
4. **"Perplexity SEO"** or **"Perplexity optimization"**
5. **"AI search optimization"**
6. **"Answer engine SEO"**

---

## How to Research (Step-by-Step)

### Method 1: Google Search Volume Check

**Use Google Keyword Planner or Ubersuggest:**

1. Go to [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/aw/keywordplanner) (free with Google Ads account)
   OR
   [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) (free tier available)

2. Enter each term and check:
   - **Monthly search volume** (higher = more popular)
   - **Competition level** (lower = easier to rank)
   - **Trend** (rising or falling?)

**What to look for:**
- Term with highest search volume = most popular
- Term with low competition = opportunity
- Term with rising trend = growing category

---

### Method 2: Google Trends Comparison

**Use [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com):**

1. Go to Google Trends
2. Compare these terms:
   - "AEO" vs "AI SEO" vs "ChatGPT SEO"
   - "Answer Engine Optimization" vs "AI SEO"
   - "ChatGPT optimization" vs "Perplexity optimization"

**What to look for:**
- Which term has higher interest over time?
- Which term is trending up?
- Which term has more regional interest?

**Example search:**
```
Compare: "AEO", "AI SEO", "ChatGPT SEO"
Time range: Past 12 months
```

---

### Method 3: Google Search Results Analysis

**For each term, search Google and document:**

1. **"AEO"** or **"Answer Engine Optimization"**
   - How many results? (shown at top: "About X results")
   - Who ranks #1-3?
   - Quality of top results?
   - Are they actual AEO content or something else?

2. **"AI SEO"**
   - How many results?
   - Who dominates?
   - Is it generic SEO content or AI-specific?

3. **"ChatGPT SEO"**
   - How many results?
   - What type of content ranks?
   - Is it actionable or theoretical?

**What to look for:**
- Fewer results = less competition = opportunity
- Lower quality results = easier to outrank
- More results = more competition = harder but proven demand

---

### Method 4: Social Media & Community Check

**Check where people actually talk about this:**

1. **Reddit:**
   - r/SEO - search each term
   - r/entrepreneur - search each term
   - r/marketing - search each term
   - Count mentions of each term

2. **LinkedIn:**
   - Search each term
   - See post engagement
   - Check who's using which term

3. **Twitter/X:**
   - Search each term
   - See recent mentions
   - Check hashtag usage

**What to look for:**
- Which term do people actually use?
- Which term gets more engagement?
- Which term is more "native" to the community?

---

### Method 5: Content Analysis

**Check what content exists for each term:**

1. **Search each term on Google**
2. **Analyze top 10 results:**
   - How comprehensive are they?
   - Do they have real examples?
   - Are they recent (2024)?
   - Do they rank well?

**What to look for:**
- Gaps in existing content = opportunity
- Low-quality content = easier to outrank
- Recent content = active category

## Expected Findings (Predictions)

### Most Likely Scenario:

**"AI SEO"** is probably the dominant term:
- ✅ More intuitive (people understand it)
- ✅ Higher search volume
- ✅ More competition
- ✅ More established

**"AEO"** is probably less common:
- ⚠️ Less intuitive (acronym)
- ⚠️ Lower search volume
- ✅ Less competition
- ✅ More opportunity to own it

**"ChatGPT SEO"** is probably growing:
- ✅ Very specific
- ✅ Rising trend
- ✅ Less competition than "AI SEO"
- ✅ Good middle ground

## Strategic Decision Framework

### Use "AEO" If:

✅ Search volume exists (even if lower)
✅ Competition is low
✅ You want to own a category
✅ You're willing to educate the market
✅ You want to be THE AEO authority

**Pros:**
- Less competition
- Category creation opportunity
- First-mover advantage (if no one owns it)
- Brandable term

**Cons:**
- Lower search volume
- Need to educate market
- Takes longer to rank
- Might not catch on

---

### Use "AI SEO" If:

✅ High search volume
✅ You want immediate traffic
✅ You can compete with quality
✅ You want proven demand

**Pros:**
- Higher search volume
- Proven demand
- People already searching
- Faster to get traffic

**Cons:**
- More competition
- Harder to rank
- Generic term
- Less brandable

---

### Use "ChatGPT SEO" If:

✅ Growing trend
✅ Specific enough to rank
✅ Less competition than "AI SEO"
✅ More specific than "AEO"

**Pros:**
- Specific and clear
- Growing category
- Less competition than "AI SEO"
- More intuitive than "AEO"

**Cons:**
- Platform-specific (ChatGPT)
- Might become outdated
- Less comprehensive term

## Recommended Strategy: Multi-Term Approach

### Don't Choose One Term - Use Multiple Strategically

**Primary Term (for branding):**
- Choose based on research
- Use consistently
- Build authority around it

**Secondary Terms (for SEO):**
- Target in content
- Use naturally in articles
- Capture different search intents

**Example Strategy:**

**Primary:** "AEO" (if you want to own the category)
**Secondary:** "AI SEO", "ChatGPT SEO" (for traffic)

**Content approach:**
- Title: "AEO: The Complete Guide to Answer Engine Optimization"
- Subtitle: "Also known as AI SEO or ChatGPT optimization"
- Content targets: "AEO", "AI SEO", "ChatGPT SEO", "Answer Engine Optimization"

- Educate market
