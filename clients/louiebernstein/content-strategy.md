- Ensure your website clearly communicates your fractional sales leadership services and value proposition
- Include clear calls-to-action (CTAs) for booking consultations or requesting more information
- Optimize your website's meta titles, descriptions, and header tags for target keywords

- Share your blog posts and other content regularly on your LinkedIn profile and company page
- Engage with your target audience by commenting on relevant posts and joining industry-specific groups

- Segment your email list based on audience demographics, interests, and engagement levels
- Personalize your email content and subject lines to improve open and click-through rates
- A/B test different email formats, CTAs, and sending times to optimize performance
- Use email automation to nurture leads and keep your audience engaged over time
