## 🎨 Design System Improvements

### Typography:
- **Headings:** Dramatically larger (5xl → 7xl/8xl)
- **Body:** Better readability with improved line-height
- **Hierarchy:** Clear size contrasts between elements

### Colors:
- **Primary:** Neutral-900 (black) for strong contrast
- **Backgrounds:** White and neutral-50 for variety
- **Accents:** Strategic use of neutral-600/700 for depth
- **Removed:** Generic primary/accent color system

### Spacing:
- **Sections:** Increased padding (py-24 → py-32)
- **Elements:** Better rhythm and breathing room
- **Grids:** Improved gap spacing

### Components:
- **Cards:** Cleaner borders (neutral-200 → neutral-900 on hover)
- **Buttons:** More prominent, better hover effects
- **Icons:** Larger, with hover animations
- **Borders:** Thicker (border-2) for more impact
