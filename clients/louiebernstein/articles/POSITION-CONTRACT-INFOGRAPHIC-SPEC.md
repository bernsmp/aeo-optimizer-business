# Position Contract ROI Infographic Specification

## Recommended Chart: ROI Comparison - Time Investment vs. Time Saved

This visual shows the compelling ROI of creating Position Contracts upfront vs. dealing with problems later.

---

## Color Scheme

**Primary Colors:**
- **Accent Blue:** `#0966c2` (Primary brand color)
- **Dark Blue:** `#0855a3` (Hover/darker accent)
- **Navy:** `#1e3a8a` (Primary text/headings)

**Neutral Colors:**
- **Background:** `#fafaf9` (Off-white)
- **Text:** `#1f2937` (Charcoal)
- **White:** `#ffffff` (Cards/contrast)

**Supporting Colors (for charts):**
- **Success Green:** `#10b981` (for positive metrics)
- **Warning Orange:** `#f59e0b` (for costs/problems)
- **Light Gray:** `#e5e7eb` (for backgrounds/separators)

---

## Infographic Content

### Title
**"The ROI of Position Contracts: 2 Hours Now Saves 20+ Hours Later"**

### Main Visual: Side-by-Side Comparison

**LEFT SIDE: "Without Position Contracts"**
- **Time Investment:** 0 hours upfront
- **Problems Created:**
  - 20-40 hours fixing performance issues
  - 5-10 hours/month in unnecessary conflict
  - 15-20 hours/month in confusion
  - 40+ hours dealing with PIPs/legal issues
- **Total Time Cost:** 80-110 hours per rep per year
- **Financial Cost:** $50,000-$150,000 per rep turnover
- **Visual:** Red/orange color scheme, downward arrows, warning icons

**RIGHT SIDE: "With Position Contracts"**
- **Time Investment:** 2-3 hours upfront
- **Problems Prevented:**
  - 0 hours fixing preventable issues
  - 0 hours in unnecessary conflict
  - 0 hours in confusion
  - 0 hours in legal issues
- **Time Saved:** 20+ hours per rep per year
- **Financial Benefit:** Prevents $50,000-$150,000 turnover costs
- **Visual:** Blue/green color scheme, upward arrows, checkmarks

### Bottom Section: Key Metrics

**"The Math"**
- **ROI:** 20:1 (2 hours saves 40+ hours)
- **Onboarding:** 50% faster (4 weeks → 2 weeks)
- **Performance:** 25-40% improvement
- **Turnover:** 3 reps/year → 0 reps/year (BankMarketingCenter.com case)

## Pro Tips

1. **Make it scannable** - Use icons, numbers, and short text
2. **Use contrast** - Dark text on light backgrounds
3. **Highlight key numbers** - Make ROI (20:1) prominent
4. **Keep it simple** - Don't overcrowd with information
5. **Brand consistency** - Use the blue accent color throughout
6. **Mobile-friendly** - Ensure it's readable on small screens
