## 🔍 Additional Recommendations

### For Full SEO Optimization:

1. **Add Schema Markup:**
   ```json
   {
     "@type": "Article",
     "headline": "135% of Quota and Put on a PIP...",
     "author": {
       "@type": "Person",
       "name": "Louie Bernstein"
     },
     "datePublished": "2025-01-23"
   }
   ```

2. **Add FAQ Schema:**
   - Convert FAQ section to structured data
   - Helps with featured snippets

3. **Add Images:**
   - Infographic showing Position Contract framework
   - Chart showing ROI of documentation
   - Author headshot

4. **Add Social Sharing:**
   - Open Graph tags
   - Twitter Card tags
   - Social sharing buttons

5. **Add Reading Time:**
   - "8 min read" indicator
   - Helps with engagement

6. **Add Table of Contents:**
   - Jump links to sections
   - Improves UX and SEO

7. **Add Comments Section:**
   - Encourages engagement
   - Shows social proof
   - Increases time on page

### For Thought Leadership:

1. **Add Original Research:**
   - Survey of 100+ sales leaders
   - Industry benchmark data
   - Trend analysis

2. **Add Video Content:**
   - 2-3 minute video summary
   - Embedded YouTube video
   - Improves engagement

3. **Add Podcast Mention:**
   - "Listen to this article" option
   - Expands reach

4. **Add Newsletter CTA:**
   - Mid-article signup
   - Exit intent popup
   - Lead generation

5. **Add Social Proof:**
   - Testimonials from readers
   - Share counts
   - Comments/quotes
