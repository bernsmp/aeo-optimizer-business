## ✨ Copywriting Principles Applied

### David Ogilvy:
- ✅ Headlines promise benefit
- ✅ Credibility markers (LinkedIn Top Voice, INC 500)
- ✅ Benefits over features

### Gary Halbert:
- ✅ Creates curiosity ("Still running sales at $1M–$10M?")
- ✅ Direct address ("You're bottlenecking")
- ✅ Clear CTA ("Ready to break through? Let's talk")

### Joe Vitale:
- ✅ Power words: "YOU", "YOUR"
- ✅ Story element ("I've scaled from zero to INC 500")
- ✅ Engaging flow

### Paul Boag:
- ✅ Information hierarchy (credential → value → CTA)
- ✅ Scannable structure
- ✅ Mobile-friendly
