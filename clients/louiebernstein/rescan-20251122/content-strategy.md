- Create 2-4 shorter blog posts or LinkedIn articles per month on subtopics 
- Produce 1 gated lead magnet asset (ebook, guide, checklist) per quarter

# Platform Optimization Tips
## Website
- Optimize website copy for target keywords related to fractional sales leadership
- Ensure site is mobile-friendly and loads quickly
- Include prominent CTAs to contact/schedule consultation 
- Feature client testimonials and logos on home page for social proof
- Create a resources section with gated and ungated sales content assets

## LinkedIn
- Share blog content and engage with target audience in relevant LinkedIn groups
- Publish native LinkedIn articles on sales leadership topics
- Optimize personal profile with keywords and client results/testimonials
- Connect with ideal client profiles and personalize outreach messages
- Run LinkedIn ad campaigns targeting job titles like Founder, CEO, Head of Sales

## YouTube
- Create a branded YouTube channel with sales training and tips videos
- Optimize video titles, descriptions, tags for target keywords 
- Use cards and end screens to drive viewers to website or consultation booking
- Embed videos in relevant blog articles to increase engagement and watch time

## Email
- Build email list by offering high-value gated content assets
- Send regular newsletter with curated sales tips and resources
- Segment email list by buyer persona and company size for targeted content
- A/B test subject lines and copy to optimize open and click-through rates
- Set up automated email nurture sequences for leads not ready to buy
