## 🏠 Editing Your Homepage

### Change Your Hero Section (Main Banner)

The hero section is the first thing visitors see - your headline, tagline, and intro video.

1. Click **"Site Settings"** in the left sidebar (under "Globals")
2. Find the **"Hero"** section
3. Edit any of these fields:
   - **Headline** - Your main title (e.g., "Fractional Sales Leader")
   - **Tagline** - Your slogan (e.g., "Less Spend. More Sales.")
   - **Description** - The paragraph below your video
   - **Video ID** - Just the YouTube video ID (the part after `v=` in a YouTube URL)
   - **CTA Buttons** - Change the text and links for your call-to-action buttons
4. Click **"Save"** in the top right

**Example:** To change your video, find a YouTube video URL like:
`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fCVKpcpD8tA`

The Video ID is: `fCVKpcpD8tA` (just the letters/numbers after `v=`)

## 📝 Managing Articles

### View All Articles

1. Click **"Articles"** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all your published articles

### Edit an Existing Article

1. Click on the article title
2. Make your changes to any field:
   - **Title** - The article headline
   - **Content** - The main body (use the rich text editor)
   - **Excerpt** - Short summary shown in previews
   - **Featured** - Toggle to feature on homepage
3. Click **"Save"**

### Create a New Article

1. Click **"Articles"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create New"** button
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** - Your article headline
   - **Slug** - URL-friendly version (e.g., "my-new-article")
   - **Content** - Write your article using the editor
   - **Excerpt** - Brief summary
   - **Status** - Set to "Published" when ready
4. Click **"Save"**

### Rich Text Editor Tips

The content editor lets you format text like a word processor:

- **Bold** - Select text and click B
- **Italic** - Select text and click I
- **Headings** - Use H2 for main sections, H3 for sub-sections
- **Bullet Lists** - Click the list icon
- **Links** - Select text and click the link icon
- **Quotes** - Use the quote block for testimonials or callouts

## 🎬 Managing Videos

### Add a New Video

1. Click **"Videos"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create New"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** - Video title
   - **YouTube ID** - Just the video ID (see example above)
   - **Description** - Brief description
   - **Category** - Choose where it appears (hero, course, shorts, playlist)
   - **Featured** - Toggle if you want it prominently displayed
4. Click **"Save"**

### Edit or Remove a Video

1. Click **"Videos"** in the sidebar
2. Click on the video you want to change
3. Make edits or click **"Delete"** to remove
4. Click **"Save"**

## 🔍 SEO Settings

Control how your site appears in Google search results:

1. Go to **Site Settings**
2. Find the **"SEO"** section
3. Edit:
   - **Site Title** - What shows in browser tabs and search results
   - **Site Description** - The description Google shows
   - **Keywords** - Words people might search for (comma-separated)
4. Click **"Save"**

## ❓ Need Help?

**Changes not showing?**
- Make sure you clicked "Save"
- Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Changes may take 1-2 minutes to appear on the live site

**Locked out?**
- Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page
- Or contact your web administrator

**Something broken?**
- Don't worry! Changes can usually be undone
- Contact your web administrator for help

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Preview before publishing** - For articles, set status to "Draft" first, preview, then change to "Published"

2. **Keep a backup** - Before making major changes, write down the current values

3. **Short and punchy** - Headlines and taglines work best when they're concise

4. **Test your links** - After updating URLs, click them to make sure they work

5. **Mobile-friendly** - Your site looks great on phones too, but keep text readable
