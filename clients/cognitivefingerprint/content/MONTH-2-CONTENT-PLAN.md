## Article 4: What Is Tacit Knowledge? (PILLAR)

### Specs
- **Target Keywords:** tacit knowledge, tacit knowledge examples, what is tacit knowledge, tacit vs explicit knowledge
- **Query Set:** implicit knowledge, unconscious knowledge, knowledge you can't explain, embodied knowledge
- **Search Intent:** Informational - learning about the concept
- **URL:** `/tacit-knowledge` (top-level for pillar authority)
- **CTA Goal:** Read cluster articles → Eventually discover CF services

### Strategic Purpose
Own the educational space. Anyone researching tacit knowledge should land here. This builds authority and captures people at the beginning of their journey.

### Key Messages
1. Tacit knowledge = what you know but can't explain
2. It's the 80-90% of expertise that's invisible
3. This is why experienced professionals struggle to teach what they do
4. Understanding this concept is the first step to extracting your expertise

### Internal Links
- Links TO: Cluster articles, "7 Signs" article, CF process page
- Links FROM: All cluster articles, homepage, about page

## Content Structure (All Hub Articles)

### Pillar Page Structure
1. Direct answer block (2-3 sentences)
2. Evidence block (academic + practical citations)
3. Comprehensive H2 sections covering full topic
4. Comparison section (tacit vs explicit)
