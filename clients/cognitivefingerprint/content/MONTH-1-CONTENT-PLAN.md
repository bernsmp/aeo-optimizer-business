## Overview

**Goal:** Establish bottom-of-funnel content that converts visitors already considering framework extraction services.

**Core Positioning:** "Create vs Extract" - Unlike courses that ask you to BUILD a framework from nothing, Cognitive Fingerprint EXTRACTS the framework you already have from your existing work.

**Target Audience:** Consultants, coaches, and experts who:
- Know they're good at what they do but can't articulate WHY
- Have tried (and failed) to create a signature framework
- Are tired of being a commodity and want to differentiate
- Want to scale beyond 1:1 work but don't know how to package their expertise

## Article 1: CF vs DIY Framework Building

### Specs
- **Target Keywords:** signature framework, create signature framework, DIY framework, framework building
- **Search Intent:** Commercial investigation - comparing approaches
- **URL:** `/blog/cognitive-fingerprint-vs-diy-framework-building`
- **CTA Goal:** Book a discovery call

### Strategic Purpose
Position the extraction approach as superior to the dominant "create from scratch" narrative. Acknowledge DIY as valid for some, but show why extraction wins for experienced experts.

### Key Messages
1. DIY works if you're early-career and still forming your approach
2. Extraction is better if you already have 5+ years of expertise
3. The framework already exists in your work - you just can't see it
4. Creation leads to generic frameworks; extraction reveals unique patterns

## Article 2: How Much Does Expert Knowledge Extraction Cost?

### Specs
- **Target Keywords:** knowledge extraction cost, framework consultant cost, signature framework pricing
- **Search Intent:** Commercial - price research before buying
- **URL:** `/blog/expert-knowledge-extraction-cost`
- **CTA Goal:** See full pricing / Book call

### Strategic Purpose
Price anchoring. Help prospects understand the investment range and value before they reach out. Position CF's pricing relative to alternatives (courses, DIY time cost, hiring consultants).

## Content Structure (All Articles)

### AIO-Ready Module
Every article includes:
1. **Direct Answer** (first 2-3 sentences) - Answers the query immediately
2. **Evidence Block** - 1-3 citations establishing credibility
3. **Question-Based H2s** - Matches how people search
4. **FAQ Section** (3-5 Qs) - With FAQ schema markup
5. **Last Updated** timestamp
6. **Named Sources** footer

### CTA Strategy
- **In-Content:** Soft CTAs after key sections ("See how this works →")
- **End-of-Article:** Clear next step (Book call, See pricing, Take assessment)
- **Exit-Intent:** Lead magnet offer (if applicable)
