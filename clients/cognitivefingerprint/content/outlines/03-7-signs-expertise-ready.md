## 4. EVIDENCE BLOCK

*1-3 citations to establish credibility. Appears after the direct answer.*

**Citation 1:**
> Research on expertise development shows that after approximately 10,000 hours of deliberate practice, knowledge becomes "tacit"—automatic and unconscious. This is why senior experts often can't teach what they know without structured extraction. (Source: K. Anders Ericsson)

**Citation 2:**
> The Dreyfus Model of Skill Acquisition identifies five stages: novice, advanced beginner, competent, proficient, and expert. At the expert level, practitioners operate from "intuition" rather than conscious rules—making self-articulation nearly impossible. (Source: Dreyfus & Dreyfus, Mind over Machine)

**Citation 3:**
> Cognitive scientists estimate that only 10-20% of expert knowledge is explicit (can be written down or easily taught). The remaining 80-90% is tacit—embodied in practice but invisible to the expert themselves. (Source: Michael Polanyi, The Tacit Dimension)

### H2: Sign #1: You've Been Doing This for 5+ Years

**Purpose:** First qualification criterion.

**Key Points:**
- Time in field = patterns have developed
- 5+ years means you've seen multiple client scenarios
- Your brain has unconsciously categorized approaches
- You've developed intuitions you don't even recognize as frameworks

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Have you been solving this type of problem long enough that it feels "automatic"?*

**What This Means:**
> If you're still in your first few years, you might need to create a framework. But after 5+ years? The patterns are there—you just haven't named them.

---

### H2: Sign #2: Clients Get Consistent Results (But You Can't Explain Why)

**Purpose:** Pattern of outcomes without conscious methodology.

**Key Points:**
- Clients achieve similar outcomes across different situations
- When asked "how did you do that?" you struggle to answer
- You might say "it depends" or "I just know"
- The consistency proves there IS a method—you just can't see it

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Can you point to a pattern of client results, even if you can't explain the exact steps?*

**Real Example:**
> "I worked with a consultant who had a 90% success rate helping executives through career transitions. When asked about her process, she said, 'I just ask the right questions.' But analysis of her transcripts revealed a precise 6-phase questioning framework she used unconsciously."

### H2: Sign #3: People Say You're "Different" (But You Don't Know Why)

**Purpose:** External validation without internal clarity.

**Key Points:**
- Clients, peers, or referrals mention your unique approach
- You've heard "You do it differently than others"
- But when pressed, you can't articulate what's different
- The difference is real—you just can't see it yourself

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Have you received feedback that you're "different" without being able to explain what makes you different?*

**What This Means:**
> External perception of uniqueness = internal patterns that distinguish you. Extraction makes those patterns visible.

---

### H2: Sign #4: You've Tried to Create a Framework and It Felt Forced

**Purpose:** Failed DIY attempts indicate extraction is the answer.

**Key Points:**
- You've taken courses or attempted self-creation
- The output felt generic, inauthentic, or "not you"
- Following templates produced something that looked like everyone else's
- You abandoned the framework because it didn't feel real

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Have you tried to build a framework only to feel like you were forcing it?*

**What This Means:**
> Creation feels forced when you're not actually creating—you're trying to invent something that already exists. Extraction feels like discovery because that's what it is.

---

### H2: Sign #5: You Repeat Certain Phrases, Questions, or Concepts with Every Client

**Purpose:** Language patterns indicate methodology.

**Key Points:**
- Clients hear the same key phrases from you
- You ask similar diagnostic questions every time
- Certain concepts or analogies show up repeatedly
- You might even be tired of hearing yourself say them

**Self-Check Question:**
> *If someone shadowed you for a week, would they notice repeated language patterns?*

**Examples to Notice:**
- "I always start by asking about X..."
- "The first thing I look for is Y..."
- "My clients hear me say Z all the time..."

**What This Means:**
> Repetition = framework. You've developed specific language because it works. That's not accident—that's methodology.

---

### H2: Sign #6: You've Been "Figuring It Out" for Clients but It Feels Intuitive

**Purpose:** Intuitive problem-solving = tacit expertise.

**Key Points:**
- Client problems feel unique but your approach is consistent
- You "figure out" solutions quickly (faster than you used to)
- The process feels intuitive, not step-by-step
- You might downplay it: "I'm just thinking through it"

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Do client problems that used to take days to solve now feel almost obvious?*

**What This Means:**
> What feels intuitive is actually a pattern-recognition system you've built unconsciously. Extraction makes that system explicit and teachable.

### H2: Sign #7: You Want to Scale but Don't Know How to "Package" Your Expertise

**Purpose:** Scale desire + packaging struggle = extraction opportunity.

**Key Points:**
- You want to create courses, train others, or license your approach
- But you don't know what to put in the curriculum
- You struggle to break down what you do into teachable steps
- You're stuck because you can't package what you can't articulate

**Self-Check Question:**
> *Have you wanted to scale beyond 1:1 but felt stuck on how to package your expertise?*

**What This Means:**
> Scaling requires a named, structured methodology. If you can't package it, extraction reveals what needs to be in the package.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**Purpose:** FAQ section with schema-ready Q&As.

#### Q1: What if I only recognize 2-3 signs?

**Answer:** You might be earlier in your expertise journey than you thought—and that's okay. Consider starting with a DIY framework-building course and revisiting extraction in 2-3 years when more patterns have developed.

#### Q2: Can I extract my own framework without help?

**Answer:** It's extremely difficult. The "curse of knowledge" means you're too close to your expertise to see patterns objectively. It's like trying to read the label from inside the bottle.

#### Q3: What if I don't have recordings or transcripts of my work?

**Answer:** You can start gathering material now. Record client calls, document your decision-making process, save email explanations. Even 5-10 detailed examples can reveal significant patterns.

#### Q4: Is extraction only for consultants?

**Answer:** No—extraction works for coaches, trainers, expert freelancers, internal subject-matter experts, and anyone whose expertise is delivered through personal interaction and decision-making.

#### Q5: How long does the extraction process take?

**Answer:** Typically 4-8 weeks depending on available source material and depth of extraction. The time investment on your end is minimal—mostly providing materials and participating in discovery sessions.
