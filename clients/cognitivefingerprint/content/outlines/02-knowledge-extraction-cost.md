### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**Purpose:** FAQ section with schema-ready Q&As.

#### Q1: Why is extraction more expensive than framework courses?

**Answer:** Courses teach you a templated process that you apply yourself over 50-200+ hours. Extraction is a done-for-you service where specialists analyze your actual work to reveal patterns you can't see yourself. The output is also fundamentally different—a unique methodology vs a templated framework.

#### Q2: Is there a payment plan available?

**Answer:** Most extraction services offer payment plans, typically split across 2-3 installments aligned with project milestones. Ask during your discovery call about options.

#### Q3: What if extraction doesn't reveal anything unique?

**Answer:** In our experience, every expert with 5+ years has patterns—uniqueness often lies in the combination of elements, not any single revolutionary idea. If truly no patterns emerge (extremely rare), reputable services offer partial refunds or alternative deliverables.

#### Q4: How do I know if I'm ready for the investment?

**Answer:** You're ready if: you have 5+ years of experience, clients get consistent results, you've tried to articulate your approach and struggled, and you're motivated to scale through training, courses, or licensing. If you're still testing your approach, start smaller or wait.

#### Q5: What materials do I need to provide?

**Answer:** Ideal materials include call transcripts (Zoom recordings work), email explanations to clients, written proposals, case study notes, and any existing documentation of your process. The more source material, the richer the extraction.
