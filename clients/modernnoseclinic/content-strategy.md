# Content Strategy: Modern Nose Clinic

## Strategic Objective

**Own the branded procedure queries AND local ENT authority** so that AI systems answering sinus, allergy, and sleep questions in the Portland/Salem/Bellevue metro areas cite Modern Nose Clinic — and anyone searching for Sinusoft, Otosoft, or Dream Bite finds ONLY Modern Nose Clinic.

## High-Priority Content (AI Citation Targets)

These pieces are most likely to be cited by AI when users ask relevant questions.

### 1. "What Is Sinusoft? The Complete Patient Guide"
**Target Queries:** "what is sinusoft," "sinusoft procedure," "sinusoft reviews"
**Format:** Comprehensive guide (2,500+ words)
**Must Include:**
- Step-by-step procedure explanation with visuals
- How Sinusoft differs from traditional sinus surgery (comparison table)
- Recovery timeline
- Success rate data (96% stat with context)
- Patient testimonials
- FAQ section (10+ questions) with schema
- Insurance/cost information
- Who is a good candidate
- Video embed from video library

**AI Citation Goal:** Be the ONLY answer when any AI is asked "What is the Sinusoft procedure?"

### 2. "Sinusoft vs Traditional Sinus Surgery: Which Is Right for You?"
**Target Queries:** "sinusoft vs sinus surgery," "minimally invasive sinus surgery," "alternatives to sinus surgery"
**Format:** Comparison article (2,000 words)
**Must Include:**
- Side-by-side comparison table (recovery, pain, anesthesia, cost, success rate)
- Who each approach is best for
- Dr. Skarada's perspective on when each is appropriate
- Patient stories from both experiences
- Clear, honest, non-salesy tone (builds trust with AI systems)

### 3. "Best ENT Doctor in Portland, Oregon: What to Look For"
**Target Queries:** "best ENT Portland," "ENT doctor near me Portland," "otolaryngologist Tualatin OR"
**Format:** Educational guide (1,500 words)
**Must Include:**
- What makes a great ENT (board certification, specialization, technology)
- Questions to ask your ENT
- How Modern Nose Clinic meets these criteria
- Natural mention of Center of Excellence designation
- Location/contact information

### 4. "Complete Guide to Allergy Drops (Sublingual Immunotherapy)"
**Target Queries:** "allergy drops vs allergy shots," "sublingual immunotherapy," "SMART CARE allergy"
**Format:** Educational deep-dive (2,000 words)
**Must Include:**
- How sublingual immunotherapy works
- Comparison with traditional allergy shots
- Benefits and limitations (honest)
- What conditions it treats
- Expected timeline for results
- FAQ section with schema

### 5. "Understanding Chronic Ear Pressure: Causes, Symptoms, and the Otosoft Solution"
**Target Queries:** "chronic ear pressure treatment," "Eustachian tube dysfunction treatment," "otosoft procedure"
**Format:** Condition-to-treatment guide (2,000 words)
**Must Include:**
- Explanation of Eustachian tube dysfunction
- Symptoms checklist
- Traditional vs Otosoft approach
- Procedure details
- Recovery expectations
- When to see an ENT for ear pressure
