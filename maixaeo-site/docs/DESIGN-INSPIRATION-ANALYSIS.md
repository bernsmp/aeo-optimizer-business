# Design Inspiration Analysis: getthinusa.com → maixAEO.com
## What Makes It Clean, Premium, and Easy to Read

**Reference Site:** https://www.getthinusa.com/
**Target Site:** maixAEO.com
**Goal:** Extract design principles for clean, premium, readable B2B design

## 🎨 Design Principles to Extract

### 1. **Typography Hierarchy**
**What to Look For:**
- Font choices (sans-serif, serif, or combination?)
- Font sizes and weight hierarchy
- Line-height and letter-spacing
- How headings vs. body text are differentiated

**Questions:**
- What font family creates that "premium" feel?
- How large is the hero headline?
- What's the ratio between H1, H2, H3, and body text?
- Is there generous whitespace around text?

**Apply to maixAEO:**
- Use similar typography hierarchy
- Ensure clear visual distinction between sections
- Maintain readability with proper line-height

---

### 2. **Color Palette & Contrast**
**What to Look For:**
- Primary color (what creates the "premium" feel?)
- Background colors (white, off-white, subtle gray?)
- Text colors (dark gray, black, or softer?)
- Accent colors (how are CTAs styled?)
- Contrast ratios (accessibility + readability)

**Questions:**
- Is it monochromatic or multi-color?
- Are there subtle gradients or flat colors?
- How are interactive elements (buttons, links) styled?
- What creates the "clean" aesthetic?

**Apply to maixAEO:**
- Choose professional color palette
- Ensure high contrast for readability
- Use accent colors strategically for CTAs

### 3. **Whitespace & Layout**

**Questions:**

- Is content centered or left-aligned?
- What's the max-width of text content?
- How are sections visually separated?

**Apply to maixAEO:**

- Constrain content width for readability
- Create clear section separation
